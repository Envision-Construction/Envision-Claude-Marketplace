## Description: <br>
Deep research harness that decomposes a question into complementary search angles, runs fan-out web searches, fetches sources, extracts falsifiable claims, adversarially verifies every claim, and synthesizes a confidence-ranked cited report. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
