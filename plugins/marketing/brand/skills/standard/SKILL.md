# Envision Brand Standard — 2026

The reference for every Envision-facing artifact. Envision is the construction-technology operator inside Prometheus Ventures. The brand is **operator-first**, not marketer-first. Precision, control, momentum — the feeling of a system working exactly as it should.

## When to use

- Writing copy for envsn.com, the OM, the manifesto, decks, emails, or HNI / investor materials
- Designing pages, dashboards, components, or marketing assets that will carry the Envision identity
- Producing logos, business cards, signage, fleet graphics, PPE, or apparel
- Reviewing existing Envision copy or visuals for brand fit
- Picking colors, typography, or photographic direction for an Envision artifact

Skip if the artifact is for another portfolio entity (Loxsle, Enspire, AEC Advancement, SCA, Atlas, Instigate, ARRC, PV Hospitality) — each has its own identity.

## Identity (verbatim — do not paraphrase)

- **Mission** — Provide an unrivaled construction experience, emphasizing cost optimization by leveraging technology-driven efficiency over antiquated industry norms.
- **Vision** — To usher construction into the modern era. While other industries adapt, a refusal to innovate undermines market efficiencies and fails the very people it's meant to serve.
- **What we do** — A transformative construction approach that delivers consistently high-quality projects through intelligent processes, optimized costs, and transparent execution.
- **Why we do it** — Construction is ~~broken~~. (The strikethrough is part of the line. Render it as a strikethrough wherever the medium allows.)
- **Archetype** — Systems Disruptor (Rebel Builder). We don't just challenge the status quo, we replace it with something better. Architects of process, developers of clarity, engineers of predictability. Where others manage projects, we build systems that make better outcomes inevitable.

### The three pillars (always in this order)

| Pillar | Frame | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Ensight** | Instant Feasibility | Real-time market data, site intelligence, and AI modeling converge to instantly validate whether a project will perform. |
| **Foresight** | Experiential Design | Integrated, data-driven design that aligns market demand, constructability, and cost to maximize user experience and NOI. |
| **Oversight** | Construction Intelligence | A single source of truth delivering real-time visibility into progress, cost, and accuracy — turning construction data into clarity and confidence. |

## Voice & tone

**We speak like operators, not marketers.** Language is grounded in reality but driven by what's next. Tone reflects control, precision, and momentum.

**The signature line:** *We design, validate, and deliver.*

### Rules

- Declarative, system-grounded sentences. Avoid hype, superlatives, and adjective stacking.
- Mechanics over adjectives — show the system, don't describe how great it feels.
- Disrupt by replacement, not by complaint. Name what we built, not what is wrong with the industry.
- Numbers are tabular, specific, and sourced. Round only at the level the audience expects.
- One idea per sentence. Cut every word that doesn't carry weight.

### Tagline — BUILD WITH INTELLIGENCE

The brand has **one tagline**, expressed two ways. They are the same positioning identity, not two competing lines.

- **Anchor form** — `BUILD WITH INTELLIGENCE.` Sits as the top-left identity on envsn.com and on every cover. Helvetica Neue Bold, ALL-CAPS, with the trailing period.
- **Diagnosis pairing** — `OUR INDUSTRY ISN'T BUILT ON TRANSPARENCY, HONESTY, OR EFFICIENCY.` Lives on the hero photograph and in the opening of any investor narrative. Pairs with the anchor form on the same surface — anchor in the identity slot, diagnosis in the hero plate. Never used as a standalone headline.

Use the anchor form alone OR the anchor + diagnosis pairing. Never stack the two as twin headlines. The diagnosis line does not exist as a standalone tagline; it only appears in service of `BUILD WITH INTELLIGENCE.`

**Deprecated:** `WHERE DEVELOPMENT MEETS DATA` appeared in the 2026 PDF as a second tagline ("Tagline II") and on the OM cover. It is no longer current. It survives only in legacy artifacts (`go.envsn.com/invest`). Do not use it in new work. There is no Tagline II — the brand's tagline is `BUILD WITH INTELLIGENCE.` in either of the two forms above.

### Anti-patterns (do not ship)

- Kitschy or playful copy. No emojis. No exclamation points outside short CTAs.
- Marketing-speak: *revolutionize, unlock, empower, seamlessly, cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, game-changing, next-generation.*
- Vague benefits ("better outcomes", "improved efficiency") with no mechanic behind them.
- Construction-industry clichés ("built different", "from the ground up", "hard hats and hustle", "we don't just build, we build relationships").
- Soft hedging ("we believe", "we strive", "we aim to", "we're committed to").
- Hyperbole or future tense as cover for missing proof ("will transform the industry").

## Color tokens

| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black | `#000000` | 0, 0, 0 | Text only — never the dark surface |
| Dark Grey | `#111111` | 17, 17, 17 | Primary dark surface (envsn.com hero, deck backgrounds, dark cards) |
| Grey | `#929296` | 146, 146, 150 | Secondary text, dividers, captions, metadata |
| Light Grey | `#E6E6E6` | 230, 230, 230 | Light surface, card background, hairlines |
| Green | `#007A53` | 0, 122, 83 | Primary brand accent — section headers, links, CTA, data highlights |
| Light Green | `#CAE8E0` | 202, 232, 224 | Tint surface, on-green text, callouts |

**Hard rules:**
- `#111111` is the default dark canvas, not pure black.
- `#007A53` is the only chromatic accent. Do not introduce blues, reds, oranges, or alternate greens.
- WCAG: Green `#007A53` passes AA on white and on `#111111` — safe for body links and CTA text.
- The palette above is the complete brand palette. Signal Yellow (below) is an exception color, not a seventh accent.

### Signal Yellow — exception color (`#F7EC13`)

Signal Yellow exists in the brand guidelines but is **not part of the working palette**. It appears in zero of the twelve reference artifacts in the 2026 PDF (cards, fleet, hard hat, vest, t-shirt, polo, logo lockups, mission page, cover, closing). Treat it as a reserved color, the way construction site signage uses yellow — for **meaning**, not decoration.

**Permitted uses (closed list — if it isn't on this list, don't):**
- Strikethrough emphasis on the canonical "Construction is ~~broken~~" line, only when the strikethrough itself needs to read at distance (large-format signage, vehicle, billboard). For body-copy contexts the strikethrough is rendered in the same color as surrounding text, not yellow.
- A single inline highlight on the `Moat` slide of the OM, where one specific phrase must read as the data point that breaks the page. One phrase per deck, one deck per audience, not a recurring device.
- Hazard / safety / "do not proceed" callouts in operational dashboards (Envision-OS error states, blocking validation failures). Pairs with iconography, not standalone.
- Print proofing marks, internal markup, and editor annotations — never visible in delivered artifacts.

**Forbidden uses:**
- As a section header, eyebrow, CTA, link, or button color.
- As a background flood for any panel, card, or surface.
- As an accent stripe, underline, or divider.
- On apparel, business cards, signage, or any printed collateral facing a client or HNI audience.
- On the website hero, marketing pages, dashboards (other than the hazard-state above), investor decks (other than the single Moat highlight above), or the OM cover.
- Anywhere green or light green would also work — green is always the first choice for emphasis.
- In any composition that already contains another instance of yellow — one yellow moment per page maximum.

**Budget rule:** if you cannot point at the specific permitted use above and name it before placing the color, don't place it. The default is no yellow.

## Typography

**One family: Helvetica Neue.** Four weights — Thin, Regular, Medium, Bold. No other typefaces in any Envision artifact.

| Role | Weight | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Display / hero number | Thin or Bold | Large hero copy, hero statistics, statement type |
| Body | Regular | Paragraph copy, captions |
| Subhead / label | Medium | Section labels, eyebrows, metadata, table headers |
| Heading / CTA | Bold | H1–H3, buttons, key callouts, the wordmark |

**Web stack:** `'Helvetica Neue', Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`. For envsn.com, serve Helvetica Neue via a licensed Adobe Fonts kit. Never substitute Inter, Geist, Manrope, or `system-ui` as the brand face.

**Rules:**
- Letter-spacing is tight: `-0.02em` to `-0.03em` for display, `-0.01em` for headings, `0` for body.
- All-caps for short labels and statements only — never set paragraph copy in all caps.
- Numerals are tabular (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) in any table or financial display.
- Line-height: `1.05` display, `1.2` headings, `1.5` body.
- No italics. The brand has no italic voice.

## Logo lockup

The mark is a stacked chevron over the wordmark **ENVISION** set in Helvetica Neue Bold.

### Single color
- Black wordmark + black icon on a light surface (`#FFFFFF` or `#E6E6E6`).
- White wordmark + white icon on a dark surface (`#111111` or `#007A53`).

### Full color
- **Default:** green icon (`#007A53`) + black wordmark on a light surface.
- Green icon (`#007A53`) + white wordmark on a dark surface.
- Light-green icon + light-green wordmark on a green surface (tonal lockup, used on apparel and tinted collateral).

### Rules
- Clear-space around the lockup = the height of the chevron mark on all sides. Nothing intrudes.
- Never recolor the icon to anything outside the palette.
- Never apply gradients, drop shadows, outer glows, bevels, or transparency to the mark.
- Never set the wordmark in any font other than Helvetica Neue Bold.
- Never separate the icon from the wordmark in primary lockups. The icon-only form is reserved for favicons, app icons, business-card fronts, hard hats, safety vests, and the polo chest.
- Minimum clear width for the full lockup: 96 px digital, 1.0 in print.

## Photography

Photography should feel like **a window into a high-functioning system** — not a staged construction shoot.

- Show what we build **and** how it works: data in action, decisions in real time, field and strategy aligned.
- Finished spaces feel precise, intentional, performance-driven — not just beautiful.
- Site photography: real crew, real PPE, mid-action. No posed group shots, no over-lit hero portraits.
- Tech photography: drones, dashboards, screens visible in-frame. Show the operating layer.
- Color grade: low-contrast, slightly cool, true-to-life. No oversaturated sunsets, no orange-and-teal cinema grade.
- Cropping: documentary framing — wide enough to read the scene, tight enough to remove staging.

## Surface specs (DESIGN — per purpose)

Every artifact maps to a surface. Each surface has a fixed spec — pick the surface first, then design within its constraints. If a new artifact does not fit a surface below, ask before inventing one.

### Digital — envsn.com & marketing

#### Hero / brand-statement surface (dark)
- **Canvas:** `#111111`.
- **Type:** Helvetica Neue Bold for headline (clamp `48–96px`, `line-height 1.05`, `letter-spacing -0.03em`). Body Regular `18–20px / 1.5 / -0.01em`.
- **Color use:** White headline. Green `#007A53` for at most one active accent — a CTA button, an underlined link, or a single emphasized word. Light Green `#CAE8E0` for secondary supporting text on dark.
- **Logo:** Full lockup, white icon + white wordmark, top-left, padding equal to icon height.
- **Photography:** Optional construction-site silhouette at `12–20%` opacity, never competing with the headline. Color grade per the Photography rules.
- **Yellow:** **None.**
- **Composition:** One headline. One supporting sentence. One CTA. No more.

#### Content / section surface (light)
- **Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` (preferred) or `#E6E6E6` (alternate band).
- **Type:** Headings Bold `32–56px`. Body Regular `16–18px / 1.5 / 0`. Eyebrows Medium ALL-CAPS `12px / letter-spacing 0.08em`.
- **Color use:** Black `#000000` body. Green `#007A53` for headings of pillar cards, links, CTA, and data accents. Grey `#929296` for metadata and dividers.
- **Logo:** Default full-color lockup (green icon + black wordmark).
- **Yellow:** **None.**
- **Composition:** Editorial grid, 12-column. Generous vertical rhythm (`80–120px` between sections). One idea per band.

#### Data / dashboard surface (Envision-OS, Foresight Studio, internal tools)
- **Canvas:** `#111111` for app shell, `#FFFFFF` cards on `#E6E6E6` board.
- **Type:** Helvetica Neue Regular `14px / 1.5 / 0` body. Medium `12px` for labels. Bold `20–32px` tabular for KPI numerals. Numerals always tabular.
- **Color use:** Green `#007A53` for positive deltas, primary CTA, active filter, selected row. Grey for inactive. Light Grey for hairlines.
- **Status colors:** Use sparing semantic colors — Green for healthy, Grey for neutral. For warning/error states use neutral typographic emphasis first (Bold + icon). If a true hazard / blocking error state needs a color cue, **Signal Yellow `#F7EC13`** is the only permitted use of yellow in the brand, paired with a clear iconographic mark and a one-line explanation. Never use red.
- **Logo:** Icon-only mark, top-left, `24–32px`.
- **Composition:** Information density is the goal. No decorative shadows, no gradient cards, no glass effects.

#### Email — internal and external
- **Canvas:** White, `600px` max width.
- **Type:** Helvetica Neue web stack. Body Regular `15px / 1.5`. Headings Bold `20–24px`.
- **Color use:** Black body. Green for links and one signature accent. No background floods.
- **Logo:** Full lockup `120px` wide, top-left of header.
- **Footer:** `envsn.com`, name, title, phone, address, in Grey `#929296` `12px Regular`.
- **Yellow:** **None.**

#### Social — LinkedIn post / Open Graph card
- **Canvas:** `#111111`. Optional one-color band of `#007A53` for category framing.
- **Type:** Bold headline `64–96px` for OG cards (1200×630). LinkedIn post copy in operator voice, ≤ 3 short paragraphs, one stat.
- **Logo:** Icon-only bottom-right, `48px`.
- **Yellow:** **None.**

### Documents — investor and HNI grade

#### OM / Manifesto / Moat (printable, HNI-defensible)
- **Canvas:** White pages with a single Dark Grey `#111111` band on the cover and section openers.
- **Type:** Body Regular `11pt / 14pt line-height`. Headings Bold `24–48pt`. Captions Medium `9pt`. Pull-quotes Thin `36–60pt` with negative letter-spacing.
- **Color use:** Black body. Green for section heads, pillar names (Ensight / Foresight / Oversight), and chart accents. Light Green for secondary chart fills. Grey for axes, footnotes, source lines.
- **Charts:** Single accent (Green) on neutral greys. No second chromatic color. Tabular numerals, two decimal places maximum, always source-cited inline (`Source: SFIP filings, 2019–2025`).
- **Logo:** Full lockup on cover, icon-only on inner pages bottom-left.
- **Yellow:** Permitted **once per deliverable**, on the Moat slide, on a single phrase that constitutes the data hit. Never on a header, never on a chart, never on a footer.
- **Photography:** Cover photo full-bleed, low-saturation construction or dashboard scene. No staged headshots in the doc body.

#### Investor deck (slides)
- **Canvas:** Mix of `#111111` (statement slides) and white (data slides). Never a green slide except as a transition card.
- **Type:** Title Bold `40–56pt`. Body Regular `18–22pt`. Statistic display Bold or Thin `96–160pt`.
- **Color use:** Same as OM. One accent per slide. No accent-on-accent.
- **Logo:** Icon-only top-left on every slide, `28px` equivalent.
- **Tagline:** Anchor form `BUILD WITH INTELLIGENCE.` on the cover. Diagnosis pairing `OUR INDUSTRY ISN'T BUILT ON TRANSPARENCY, HONESTY, OR EFFICIENCY.` on the problem slide. Never as twin headlines on the same slide.
- **Yellow:** Same constraint as the OM — one Moat phrase, deck-wide.

#### Email signature (block)
- **Type:** Name in Bold ALL-CAPS, title in Medium, contact in Regular `11pt`. `envsn.com` rendered in green.
- **Logo:** Icon-only left of the name block, `40px`.
- **No QR code, no banner image, no quote.**

### Physical — collateral

#### Business card
- **Front:** Green flood (`#007A53`) with the icon-only mark in white, optional dark construction photo at `15–25%` opacity underlay. No name on front.
- **Back:** White card. Full lockup top-left. Name in Bold ALL-CAPS `10pt`, title in Medium `8pt`, phone + email in Regular `8pt`. QR code bottom-right `0.75in`. `envsn.com` link rendered in green Bold.
- **Stock:** Heavy matte (`16–24pt`), no gloss, soft-touch coating preferred.
- **Yellow:** **None.**

#### Fleet (vehicles)
- **Body:** Black truck. Vinyl print / laminate / cut applied graphics.
- **Driver + passenger door:** White full lockup, centered, height = `40%` of door panel.
- **Tailgate:** `envsn.com` in white Bold ALL-CAPS, centered, `2in` tall.
- **Grille:** Optional icon-only inset.
- **Yellow:** **None.**

#### Hard hat
- **Shell:** Black carbon-fiber.
- **Front:** White icon screen-printed, `2.5in` tall.
- **Side:** White wordmark `ENVISION` in Bold, `1.25in` tall.
- **Yellow:** **None.** (Reflective tape stripes if required by safety code are a regulatory choice, not a brand choice — keep tape clear of the icon.)

#### Safety vest
- **Body:** Envision green `#007A53` mesh.
- **Tape:** Silver + lime reflective bands per ANSI/ISEA 107. The lime tape is a safety requirement, not the brand's Signal Yellow.
- **Mark:** White icon screen-printed center back only, `8in` tall. No wordmark.
- **Yellow:** Brand yellow is **never added** to safety apparel. Lime reflective tape is the only yellow-adjacent element and exists for OSHA compliance.

#### T-shirt — statement
- **Body:** Black.
- **Front:** White wordmark `ENVISION` across the chest, Bold ALL-CAPS, `1.5in` tall.
- **Back:** `NO SMOKE / NO MIRRORS / JUST RESULTS` stacked, Bold ALL-CAPS, strikethrough on `NO` `SMOKE` `NO` `MIRRORS`. Icon below, `1.5in`. `ENVSN.COM` at the bottom, Bold ALL-CAPS, `0.75in`.
- **Yellow:** **None.**

#### Polo — quiet
- **Body:** Black.
- **Chest:** Embroidered icon-only left chest, `2in` tall, tonal black-on-black thread or white thread. No wordmark.
- **Yellow:** **None.**

#### Site signage / jobsite hoarding
- **Canvas:** Dark Grey `#111111` ground.
- **Logo:** White full lockup, scaled to read from the sidewalk distance.
- **Tagline:** Anchor form `BUILD WITH INTELLIGENCE.` in white Bold. Period is part of the line.
- **Project info:** Address, project name, completion date in Medium ALL-CAPS, white on dark.
- **Yellow:** **None on brand signage.** OSHA-required safety signage (separate boards) keeps its mandated yellow and is not a brand surface.

## Common mistakes

- Using `#000000` for dark surfaces instead of `#111111` — flattens the brand.
- Setting body copy in green — green is accent only, not text-at-length.
- Introducing a second typeface ("Inter for the web, Helvetica for print") — there is no second typeface.
- Writing in marketing voice ("revolutionizing construction!") instead of operator voice.
- Stripping the strikethrough from "Construction is broken" — the strikethrough is the statement.
- Empty-jobsite photography with no people, screens, or data context.
- Stacking the anchor form and the diagnosis pairing as twin headlines on the same surface. They are anchor + hero plate, not co-headlines.
- Using the diagnosis pairing as a standalone tagline (without `BUILD WITH INTELLIGENCE.` on the same surface). The diagnosis only exists in service of the anchor.
- Calling the brand's tagline anything other than `BUILD WITH INTELLIGENCE.` There is no "Tagline II." The two expressions are forms of one identity.
- Using the deprecated `WHERE DEVELOPMENT MEETS DATA` line in new work. It is grandfathered into existing OM artifacts only.
- Recoloring the chevron icon to match a section ("a blue one for tech, a red one for risk"). The icon is green or monochrome — never anything else.
- Using emojis or ALL-CAPS exclamations in body copy.
- **Treating Signal Yellow `#F7EC13` as a sixth accent color.** It is an exception color. If you are reaching for yellow as a section background, a button, a header, a divider, a chart series, or a callout box, you are using it wrong. Use green instead.
- **Reaching for yellow because "we need something else besides green."** The brand is intentionally monochromatic + green. The discipline of one accent is the brand. Reaching for another accent is the failure mode the brand exists to prevent.
- **Using yellow on apparel, business cards, signage, or any HNI-facing collateral.** Zero of the twelve reference artifacts in the 2026 PDF use yellow. Match that posture.
- **Multiple yellow moments on the same page.** If the Moat slide already has one yellow highlight, no other element on the deck — header, chart, footer, divider — may also be yellow.
- Exposing `--envision-signal-yellow` or `envision.highlight` as a Tailwind / theme token. If a component truly needs it, inline the hex literal so the choice surfaces in code review.
