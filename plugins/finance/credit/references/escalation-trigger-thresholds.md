# Escalation Trigger Thresholds

**Last Updated:** March 2026
**Update Cadence:** Annually
**Next Review:** March 2027

This file consolidates all numeric thresholds used in credit surveillance escalation. It serves as the single source of truth for both the governance escalation tiers (Tier 1-4) and the surveillance classification system (Green/Yellow/Orange/Red). Skill-level references describe the process and framework; this file contains the numbers.

---

## 1. Governance Escalation Tiers (Tier 1-4)

### Tier 1: Analyst Watch

**Response Time:** 5 business days from identification

| Trigger Category | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Spread widening | >100 bps vs. entry or rating cohort |
| Rating action | 1-notch downgrade or outlook change to negative |
| Covenant headroom | Compresses below 20% |
| EBITDA variance | >5% below model on LTM basis |
| Liquidity runway | Falls below 18 months |
| Loan price decline | 3-5 points from entry (idiosyncratic) |

### Tier 2: Team Review

**Response Time:** 3 business days from identification

| Trigger Category | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Spread widening | >200 bps vs. entry or rating cohort |
| Rating action | Multi-notch downgrade (2+ notches) |
| Covenant breach | Probable within 2 quarters based on current trajectory |
| EBITDA variance | >10% below model for 2+ consecutive quarters |
| Liquidity runway | Falls below 12 months |
| Free cash flow | Negative for 2 consecutive quarters |
| Loan price decline | >5 points from entry (idiosyncratic) |

### Tier 3: IC Notification

**Response Time:** 2 business days from identification; IC discussion at next scheduled meeting (or ad-hoc if >5 business days away)

| Trigger Category | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Covenant breach | Actual breach, waiver request, or amendment initiated |
| Default event | Technical default on any obligation |
| LME announcement | Liability management exercise proposed or rumored |
| Realized loss | >5% of original position market value |
| Rating action | Downgrade to CCC+ or below |
| CDS spread | Exceeds 500 bps |
| Conditional approval | Any IC condition remains unverified at trade date |

### Tier 4: Emergency IC

**Response Time:** Immediate — same business day, emergency IC meeting within 24 hours

| Trigger Category | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Payment default | Missed interest or principal payment |
| Bankruptcy filing | Chapter 11, Chapter 7, or foreign equivalent |
| Fraud discovery | Material misrepresentation or restatement |
| Liquidity exhaustion | <30 days cash runway, no committed facilities |
| Cross-default | Triggered on any obligation in the capital structure |

## 2. Response Time Summary

| Escalation | Maximum Response Time | IC Involvement |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 — Analyst Watch | 5 business days | None (PM informed) |
| Tier 2 — Team Review | 3 business days | Written notification |
| Tier 3 — IC Notification | 2 business days | Formal IC discussion and binding decision |
| Tier 4 — Emergency IC | Same business day | Emergency meeting within 24 hours |

## 3. Surveillance Classification Thresholds (Green/Yellow/Orange/Red)

### Green (Performing)

All of the following conditions are met:

| Dimension | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Financial metrics | Within 10% of underwriting projections |
| Covenant headroom | Above 25% |
| Interest coverage | Above 2.5x |
| Liquidity runway | Exceeds 18 months |
| Market price / spread | Stable relative to rating cohort |
| Qualitative | No adverse news, rating actions, or management changes |

### Yellow (Watch)

Any ONE of the following quantitative triggers:

| Dimension | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Net leverage | Increases more than 0.5x above underwriting case |
| EBITDA variance | Declines more than 5% below model on LTM basis |
| Covenant headroom | Compresses to 15-25% |
| Free cash flow | Negative for one quarter (when model projected positive) |
| Interest coverage | Declines below 2.5x |
| Liquidity runway | Falls to 12-18 months |
| Loan price | Declines 3-5 points from entry (idiosyncratic) |
| Credit spread | Widens 100-200 bps relative to entry or peers |

### Orange (Concern)

Any ONE of the following quantitative triggers:

| Dimension | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Net leverage | Exceeds underwriting case by more than 1.0x |
| EBITDA variance | Declines more than 10% below model on LTM basis for 2+ consecutive quarters |
| Covenant headroom | Compresses below 15% (within 10% of breach) |
| Free cash flow | Negative for 2 consecutive quarters |
| Interest coverage | Declines below 1.75x |
| Liquidity runway | Falls below 12 months |
| Loan price | Declines more than 5 points from entry (idiosyncratic) |
| Credit spread | Widens more than 200 bps from entry |
| CDS spread | Exceeds 500 bps |
| Rating action | Downgrade by one or more notches |

### Red (Action Required)

Any ONE of the following quantitative triggers:

| Dimension | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Covenant breach | Actual breach or technical default |
| Payment default | Missed interest or principal payment |
| Cross-default | Triggered on any obligation |
| Net leverage | Exceeds underwriting case by more than 2.0x |
| Interest coverage | Below 1.0x (cash interest exceeds EBITDA) |
| Liquidity runway | Falls below 6 months |
| Loan price | Below 80 (absent market-wide dislocation) |
| CDS spread | Exceeds 1,000 bps |
| Rating action | Downgraded to CCC+ or below |

## 4. Tier-to-Color Mapping

The governance escalation tiers and surveillance color classifications are complementary systems. The following table maps how they align:

| Surveillance Color | Primary Governance Tier | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Green | No escalation | Credit performing as expected; standard quarterly surveillance |
| Yellow | Tier 1 (Analyst Watch) | Early warning signals; analyst-led monitoring with PM notification |
| Orange | Tier 2 (Team Review) / Tier 3 (IC Notification) | Material deterioration; team review escalating to IC involvement as severity increases |
| Red | Tier 3 (IC Notification) / Tier 4 (Emergency IC) | Imminent or actual default events; mandatory IC engagement and potential emergency action |

**Notes on overlap:**
- A credit can be classified Orange and sit at Tier 2 if the deterioration is financial but no covenant breach or default event has occurred. It escalates to Tier 3 once a breach, default, or LME is triggered.
- A credit classified Red always requires at least Tier 3. It reaches Tier 4 when a payment default, bankruptcy, fraud, or liquidity exhaustion is confirmed.
- Color classification drives monitoring frequency and documentation requirements. Tier classification drives the governance response (who is involved and decision authority).

## 5. Escalation Timeline Thresholds

| Tier Change | Maximum Response Time |
|---|---|
| Green to Yellow | 5 business days from trigger identification |
| Yellow to Orange | 3 business days from trigger identification |
| Orange to Red | 24 hours from trigger identification |
| Any tier to Red (payment default or fraud) | Immediate — same business day |

## 6. De-Escalation Numeric Criteria

### Red to Orange
- Liquidity runway restored to at least 6 months
- Minimum observation period: 30 days post-cure

### Orange to Yellow
- Covenant headroom rebuilt above 15%
- EBITDA trajectory flat or improving for at least 1 quarter
- Minimum observation period: 1 full quarter post-stabilization

### Yellow to Green
- Financial metrics within 10% of underwriting case for at least 2 consecutive quarters
- Covenant headroom above 25%
- Minimum observation period: 2 full quarters post-resolution

## 7. Recalibration Guidance

These thresholds should be reviewed and potentially recalibrated under the following conditions:

- **Volatility regime change:** If market-wide spread volatility increases significantly (e.g., CDX HY implied vol sustained above historical 75th percentile), spread-based triggers may need widening to avoid excessive false positives
- **Strategy or portfolio mandate shift:** Changes to target credit quality, sector concentration limits, or return targets may require tighter or looser thresholds
- **Post-mortem findings:** If post-mortem reviews reveal that escalation triggers were systematically too late or too early, adjust the relevant thresholds at the next annual review
- **Regulatory or LP requirements:** Changes in fund documentation, regulatory guidance, or LP reporting expectations may impose minimum escalation standards
- **Market cycle positioning:** Thresholds calibrated in a benign credit environment may be too tight in a downturn; consider regime-dependent overlays for spread and price triggers
