# Typical Deal Parameters & Structural Conventions

**Update Cadence:** Annually

---

## 1. LBO Entry Leverage

| Deal Size | Typical Leverage | Equity % | Senior Debt % |
|-----------|------------------|----------|---------------|
| Large Cap ($10B+ EBITDA) | 4.0–4.5x | 30–40% | 70–60% |
| Upper MM ($500M–$2B EBITDA) | 5.0–6.0x | 25–35% | 75–65% |
| Core MM ($100M–$500M EBITDA) | 5.5–6.5x | 20–30% | 80–70% |
| Lower MM ($25M–$100M EBITDA) | 6.0–7.5x | 15–25% | 85–75% |

**Equity Contribution Norms:** Sponsors typically target 25–40% equity at entry; excess leverage funded via TLA and TLB. Mezzanine financing bridges 15–25% of capital structure in lower MM deals.

## 2. Loan Structure Conventions

| Parameter | Institutional | Middle Market |
|-----------|---------------|----------------|

| **TLA vs TLB Split** | 40–50% TLA / 50–60% TLB | 50–60% TLA / 40–50% TLB |
| **TLB Amortization** | 1% per annum | 5–10% per annum |
| **TLA Maturity** | 5–7 years | 5–6 years |
| **TLB Maturity** | 6–7 years | 6–7 years |
| **Bond Maturity** | 7–10 years (for larger deals) | N/A typical |

## 4. Call Protection & Repricing

**TLB Call Protection:**
- **Soft Call:** 101 for first 6–12 months (101-2), declining 0.5–1.0 point per quarter
- **Hard Call:** After soft call period, issued at par (100)

**Bond Call Schedules:**
- **NC/2:** No call for 2 years, then step-down (101 yr 3, 100.5 yr 4, par thereafter)
- **NC/3:** No call for 3 years; step-down structure per issuer preference

**Make-Whole Provisions:** Bonds typically include make-whole language allowing early redemption at greater of par or PV of remaining coupons + yield spread.

## 5. Covenant Conventions

| Maintenance Covenant | Typical Initial Level | Step-Down Target |
|---------------------|----------------------|------------------|
| **Leverage Ratio** | 5.0–5.5x | 4.0–4.5x (by exit) |
| **Interest Coverage** | 2.0–2.5x | 3.0–3.5x (by exit) |
| **Minimum EBITDA** | Used in some small MM deals | N/A |

**Headroom:** Sponsors maintain 15–25% buffer above maintenance covenants in Year 1. Step-downs typically occur annually or linked to leverage reduction milestones.

**Incurrence Tests:** Restricted payments, acquisitions, and debt issuances often require leverage <4.5x (or pro forma <4.75x) and interest coverage >2.5x.

**Restricted Payment Baskets:** Typically 50% of Excess Cash Flow or cumulative EBITDA after capex, debt paydown, and working capital.

## 6. M&A Deal Parameters

| Metric | Typical Range |
|--------|----------------|
| **Control Premium** | 20–40% (depends on market conditions) |
| **Break-Up Fee** | 2.0–4.0% of Enterprise Value |
| **Reverse Break-Up Fee** | 1.0–3.0% of Enterprise Value |
| **Go-Shop Period** | 30–60 days post-signing |
| **Financing Contingency** | Rare for debt; common for equity in lower MM |

**Termination Risk:** Deal certainty premium widest for smaller, sponsor-led transactions (100–150 bps); narrows for large cap strategic M&A.

## 7. PE Fund & Sponsor Norms

| Metric | Target Range |
|--------|---------------|
| **Gross IRR** | 20–25% |
| **Net IRR (after fees)** | 15–20% |
| **MOIC (Money Multiple)** | 2.0–3.0x |
| **Typical Hold Period** | 3–7 years |
| **Exit Multiples** | +0.5–1.5x relative to entry (dependent on sector) |

**Fee Structure:** 2% management fee on committed capital (years 1–5), declining after; 20% carry with 8–10% hurdle. Co-invest terms typically waive carry on co-invested capital in lower MM.

## 9. Trading & Settlement

| Metric | Standard |
|--------|----------|
| **Par Trading Settlement** | T+7 (LSTA standard for syndicated loans) |
| **Distressed/Non-Performing** | T+20 (extended for below-par secondary trading) |
| **Assignment Fee** | $3,500 (LSTA standard) |
| **Delayed Compensation** | SOFR + spread from T+7 to settlement (20–60 bps typical) |

**Participation vs Assignment:** Primary market typically assignment; secondary often participation. Participation preserves relationship, no fee; assignment transfers legal title, requires consent.

## 10. SOFR Floor Conventions

| Loan Type | Typical Floor | Rationale |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| **Broadly Syndicated (Large Cap)** | 0.50–1.00% | Lower cost, tighter syndication |
| **Upper MM Institutional** | 0.75–1.00% | Moderate credit risk |
| **Core/Lower MM** | 0.75–1.25% | Higher execution risk, sponsor credit |

**Floor Value:** Estimated at 5–10 bps per 0.25% floor increment in economic value (rough proxy: floor value ≈ 0.4 × floor % / spread %). Used in pricing negotiations and amendment negotiations.

## 11. Sustainability-Linked Loan Conventions

| Parameter | Typical Convention | Notes |
|-----------|--------------------|-------|
| **KPI Count** | 2–4 KPIs | Too few can miss material risks; too many dilute accountability |
| **Margin Ratchet** | +/- 5 to 25 bps | Leveraged loan deals often cluster around +/- 7.5 to 12.5 bps |
| **Testing Frequency** | Annual | More frequent testing is uncommon; less frequent testing weakens discipline |
| **Structure** | Symmetric step-up / step-down preferred | One-way discounts are viewed as weak and can raise greenwashing concerns |
| **Verification** | Annual external verification | Limited assurance is common; reasonable assurance is stronger where feasible |
| **Documentation** | Sustainability schedule or pricing schedule annex | Definitions, methodology changes, and non-reporting consequences should be explicit |

**Common KPI Types:** Emissions intensity, safety metrics, water intensity, waste metrics, and selected workforce diversity metrics when directly linked to sector risk.

**Analytical Use:** Treat these as current market conventions, not timeless rules. Sector fit, borrower profile, and lender composition can shift where the market clears.

## 12. CRE Deal Parameters

### LTV / DSCR / Debt Yield Norms by Lender Type

| Lender Type | Max LTV | Min DSCR | Min Debt Yield | Typical Rate Type | Typical Term | Amortization | Recourse |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Agency (Fannie/Freddie) | 75% | 1.25x | N/A | Fixed 10-yr | 10 yrs | 30-year | Non-recourse (with exceptions) |
| CMBS Conduit | 80% | 1.20x | 3.0–3.5% | Fixed 7–10 yr | 7–10 yrs | 25–30 year | Limited recourse |
| Life Insurance Co | 75% | 1.30x | 2.8–3.2% | Fixed 10–15 yr | 10–15 yrs | 25–30 year | Non-recourse |
| Bank Portfolio | 80% | 1.15x | 2.5–3.0% | SOFR+spread | 5–7 yrs | 25–30 year | Recourse to sponsor |
| Bridge/Debt Fund | 85% | 1.10x (I/O) | 6.0–8.0% | SOFR+spread | 2–3 yrs | Interest-only | Recourse to sponsor |
| Hard Money | 70–75% | 1.00x (I/O) | 8.0–12.0%+ | SOFR+spread | 12–24 mos | Interest-only | Full recourse |

**Context:** Agency lenders most conservative; bridge/hard money provide construction/transition flexibility at premium costs. DSCR floors lower for bridge/I/O structures.

### Standard Amortization Schedules

- **25-year Amortization:** Most common for permanent CRE financing; reduces amortization burden, front-loaded interest deductions
- **30-year Amortization:** Standard for Agency multifamily (GSE-eligible); extends balloon period for CMBS (may have 10-year amortization with 20-year balloon)
- **Interest-Only (I/O):** Standard for bridge loans; construction phase typically 1–3 years; some permanent loans feature 2–3 year I/O step-in periods

## Structured Finance Deal Parameters

Standard terms, structural conventions, and leverage/coverage metrics for project finance, securitization, and asset finance structures.

### Project Finance Deal Parameters

**Typical Leverage & Coverage Targets**:

| Metric | Conservative | Market Standard | Aggressive |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|-----------|
| Senior Debt/Total Capitalization | 65–70% | 70–75% | 75–85% |
| DSCR (Minimum, Contracted Revenue) | 1.30–1.40x | 1.20–1.30x | 1.15–1.20x |
| DSCR (Minimum, Demand Risk) | 1.40–1.50x | 1.30–1.40x | 1.20–1.30x |
| LLCR (Loan Life Coverage) | 1.40–1.50x | 1.20–1.40x | 1.10–1.20x |
| PLCR (Project Life Coverage) | 1.35–1.45x | 1.25–1.35x | 1.15–1.25x |

**Debt Term Structure**:
- Bank debt tenor: 12–20 years (12–15 typical for PPP, 15–20 for utility/infrastructure)
- Bond tenor: 15–25 years (longer tenor available; lower refinancing risk)
- Refinancing risk: minimal if DSCR/coverage remain strong through maturity; consider balloon risk if debt not amortized

**Contingency & Reserve Sizing**:
- Construction contingency: 5–10% of EPC cost (lender-funded or sponsor-funded)
- DSRA (Debt Service Reserve Account): 6–12 months forward debt service (6 months typical; 12 months in high-risk projects)
- Maintenance reserve: project-dependent; utility infrastructure may require 2–3% of annual OpEx annually

### Asset-Based Lending (ABL) Parameters

**Advance Rates** (% of collateral value):

| Collateral Type | Conservative | Market | Aggressive |
|-----------------|--------------|--------|-----------|
| Accounts Receivable | 75–80% | 80–85% | 85–90% |
| Inventory | 40–50% | 50–65% | 65–75% |
| Equipment (OLV) | 50–60% | 60–75% | 75–85% |
| Fixed Assets / Real Estate | 50–60% | 60–70% | 70–80% |

**Pricing**: See `references/market-benchmarks.md` for current ABL spread and pricing levels.

**Structural Norms**:
- Revolver commitment fee: 25–50 bps (on undrawn capacity)
- FILO (First-In-Last-Out) spread premium: typically +200–400 bps over revolver coupon
- Field exam frequency: 1–2x per year (cost: $3K–$15K per exam depending on collateral complexity)

**Covenant Packages**:
- Leverage ratio: 2.5–3.5x Total Debt/EBITDA (maximum)
- Interest coverage: 2.0–2.5x EBITDA/Interest (minimum)
- Asset coverage: Borrowing base ≥ 110–125% of outstanding borrowings (minimums vary by lender)
- Working capital minimum: sometimes required (e.g., DPO >30 days = increased credit risk)

### Equipment and Specialty Asset Finance Parameters

Use these ranges as current market conventions rather than timeless underwriting rules. Always adjust for asset quality, useful life, maintenance condition, and secondary-market depth.

**General Equipment Finance / Leasing**:

| Metric | Conservative | Market Standard | Aggressive |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|-----------|
| Loan Term | 3–5 years | 5–7 years | 7+ years |
| Balloon / Residual as % of Original Cost | 0–15% | 15–35% | 35%+ |

See `references/market-benchmarks.md` for current equipment finance spread levels.

**Specialty Asset Finance by Asset Class**:

| Asset Class | Conservative Leverage | Market Standard | Aggressive | Notes |
|------------|-----------------------|-----------------|-----------|------|
| Aircraft | 60–70% of appraised value | 70–80% | 80%+ | Newer, liquid narrowbody fleets clear at the higher end; older or less fungible aircraft need more cushion |
| Shipping | 50–60% of appraised value | 60–70% | 70%+ | Cyclicality, charter profile, and age drive where within the band a deal clears |
| Rolling Stock / Rail | 65–75% of appraised value | 75–80% | 80–85% | Longer-lived assets can support longer tenor if maintenance and lease placement remain strong |

**Residual / Remarketing Considerations**:
- Stronger markets allow more balloon or residual support; weaker markets require faster amortization.
- Assets with fast technological change, narrow buyer pools, or regulatory retrofit risk should price and leverage inside the conservative end of the range.
- Where repayment depends on a future sale or re-lease, underwrite the refinance path separately from the headline LTV.

### Securitization Parameters

**Risk Retention** (Dodd-Frank / Basel III):
- US ABS: Issuer must retain minimum 5% of credit risk
- Forms: first-loss position, subordinated tranches, IO strips, overcollateralization
- Example: $100M auto ABS securitized; issuer retains $5M first-loss equity or subordinated B tranche

**Subordination Levels** (AAA tranches):

| Asset Type | Subordination % | Notes |
|------------|-----------------|-------|
| Auto Prime | 5–8% | Strong collateral; low LGD |
| Auto Subprime | 25–35% | High default risk; substantial cushion |
| Credit Card | 10–15% | Stable pools; moderate risk |
| Student Loan | 5–8% | Government backing reduces risk |
| Equipment Lease | 8–12% | Collateral-backed; moderate risk |
| RMBS Non-Agency | 20–30% | Variable based on vintage, geography |
| CMBS Conduit | 20–30% | Property-type dependent |

**Servicing Fees**:

| Product | Servicing Fee | Master Servicer | Special Servicer |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|------------------|
| Auto ABS | 0.25–0.50% of WAL | 0.05–0.10% | N/A |
| Credit Card ABS | 0.25–0.35% | 0.05% | N/A |
| RMBS | 0.25% (master) + 0.25% (servicer) | Varies | 0.35–0.75% for delinquent |
| CMBS | 0.35–0.50% base | 0.05–0.10% | 0.75–1.25% if >delinquency threshold |

**Expected Loss Levels** (Modeled):

| Asset Type | Base Case Default | Recovery Rate | Expected Loss % |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------|-----------------|
| Auto Prime | 0.8–1.5% | 75–85% | 0.15–0.40% |
| Auto Subprime | 5–7% | 60–70% | 1.5–2.8% |
| Credit Card | 2–3% | 70–80% | 0.40–0.90% |
| Equipment | 1–2% | 80–90% | 0.10–0.40% |
| RMBS (Prime) | 0.5–1.0% | 80–90% | 0.05–0.20% |
| CMBS (Conduit) | 1–2% | 75–85% | 0.15–0.50% |

**Trigger Testing**:
- Early amortization triggers: Common Loss Level >X%, Delinquency >Y%, Spread compression
  - Example: if cumulative defaults >6% of original pool or delinquencies >15%: shift to amortization
  - Impact: all principal redirected to senior tranches; equity receives nothing until recovery
- Interest shortfall: if spread insufficient to cover investor interest, draw from reserve/subordinated tranches
- Servicer replacement: if servicer financial metrics degrade or performance misses targets

### Typical Deal Structures

**Large-Cap Project Finance** ($2B+ project):
- 75% leverage (25% equity)
- Senior debt: 70% of capital (12–15 year tenor, bank debt)
- Subordinated debt: 5% of capital (mezzanine, longer tenor)
- DSCR minimum: 1.25x
- Typical LLCR: 1.25–1.35x

**Mid-Market LBO** ($500M–$2B EBITDA):
- 5.5–6.5x leverage
- TLB: 6–7 year tenor
- Revolver: 5–6 year tenor
- Interest coverage: 2.0–2.5x
- Leverage step-downs if EBITDA growth achieved
- See `references/market-benchmarks.md` for current spread levels

**ABL Securitization** (Equipment / Receivables):
- Advance rate: 70–80% of collateral
- Subordination: 12–20% for AAA tranches
- Servicer fee: 0.35–0.50%
- Average life: 3–5 years (collateral turnover)
- Trigger testing: quarterly on loss ratios, delinquency

---

**Note on Market Evolution**: Leverage and spreads are subject to credit cycle dynamics. Expect compression in spreads and leverage in booming periods; widening and deleveraging in contractions. See `references/market-benchmarks.md` for current market conditions.
