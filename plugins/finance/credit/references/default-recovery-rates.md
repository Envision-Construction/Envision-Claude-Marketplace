## 8. CRE Default & Recovery Rates

### CMBS Delinquency Rates by Property Type (%)

| Property Type | Current (Q1 2026) | 2025 Avg | 2024 Avg | Trend |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Overall CMBS | 3.5–4.0% | 3.8% | 4.2% | Stabilizing |
| Multifamily | 1.0–2.0% | 1.5% | 1.8% | Resilient; strong demand |
| Industrial | 0.8–1.8% | 1.2% | 1.5% | Lowest; e-commerce tailwind |
| Retail | 5.5–7.5% | 6.5% | 7.0% | Structural shift; stabilizing |
| Office | 7.0–9.5% | 8.5% | 9.0% | Elevated; remote work impact |
| Hospitality | 4.0–6.5% | 5.0% | 5.5% | Normalizing post-pandemic |
| Mixed-Use | 3.0–5.0% | 4.0% | 4.5% | Mid-range; use-specific risk |

**Office Stress:** Extended elevated delinquencies due to structural occupancy declines, tenant downsizing. Expect gradual improvement as buildings reposition to mixed-use or adapt for flexible work.

### CRE Loan Loss Severity by Property Type (Loss Severity = 1 − Recovery Rate)

| Property Type | Loss Severity | Recovery Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multifamily | 20–30% | 70–80% | Strong collateral values; active investor base |
| Industrial | 15–25% | 75–85% | High appeal; often re-leases quickly |
| Office | 35–50% | 50–65% | Structural obsolescence risk; harder to reposition |
| Retail | 30–45% | 55–70% | Anchored assets better; unanchored higher loss |
| Hospitality | 25–40% | 60–75% | Operating business risk; collateral dependent |

**Methodology:** Loss severity reflects (a) liquidation timeline, (b) tenant credit & occupancy at time of default, (c) property-type specific demand, (d) market cycles. Industrial most liquid; office liquidity constrained.

### CRE Workout & Resolution Timelines

| Resolution Type | Timeline | Typical Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Loan Modification (forbearance) | 3–6 months | Extend maturity, reduce payment, amend covenants |
| Deed-in-Lieu (DIL) | 2–4 months | Lender takes title; avoid foreclosure costs |
| Non-Judicial Foreclosure | 2–6 months | Faster in non-judicial states (CA, AZ, NV, TX) |
| Judicial Foreclosure | 6–18 months | Slower; required in some states; creditor defenses |
| CMBS Special Servicing Resolution | 12–24 months | Extended ramp-up; servicer negotiations, workout plans |

**Bridge vs Permanent:** Bridge loans default faster (12–24 mos typical); permanent loans have longer forbearance windows (6–12 mos typical before acceleration).

### Historical CRE Default Rates (Annual)

| Period | Default Rate | Context |
|---|---|---|
| Long-term Average (2000–2025) | 2.0–3.5% | Normalized steady-state |
| 2019–2020 (COVID) | 1.5–4.0% | Spike in hospitality; most others resilient |
| 2008–2012 (GFC) | 8.0–12.0% | Peak distress; multifamily hit hardest |
| 2025–2026E (Current) | 1.5–2.5% | Moderate; localized stress in office |

**Banked Loan Averages:** ~2–3% annual default for bank-originated CRE mortgages (FDIC data); CMBS conduits historically 2–4% cumulative by vintage (5-year performance).

**Office Vulnerability:** Post-2020, office vacancy and concessions triggered elevated defaults (peaks 8%+); multifamily and industrial remained 1–2% throughout. Expect office delinquencies to trend toward 5–6% range over 3 years as market equilibrates.
