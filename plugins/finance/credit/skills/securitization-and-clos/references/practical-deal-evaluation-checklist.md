## Part 10: Practical Deal Evaluation Checklist

### Key Principles

#### General Structured-Credit Principles

1. **Structure first**: If you do not understand the waterfall, you do not understand the bond.
2. **Enhancement is not a substitute for collateral quality**: Subordination buys time, not immunity.
3. **Timing matters as much as magnitude**: The same cumulative loss can produce very different tranche outcomes depending on when defaults arrive.
4. **Optionality has a cost**: Prepayments, calls, and extension risk can overwhelm simple spread comparisons.
5. **Servicing quality is credit quality**: Weak servicing, slow workouts, or conflicted special servicing can destroy realized value.

#### CLO-Specific Principles

1. **OC and IC tests are the guardrails**: Cushion, direction of travel, and manager behavior around the tests matter more than a single snapshot.
2. **Manager underwriting and trading discipline matter most when the market is stressed**: Benign-vintage track records should not be treated as proof of downside skill.
3. **Equity is a leveraged residual claim, not a high-coupon bond**: Cash yield without NAV discipline can be misleading.
4. **Mezzanine tranches are hurt first by trapped cash and haircut mechanics**: They need more than a simple spread pickup to compensate for structural volatility.
5. **Senior CLO debt is usually a spread-and-liquidity decision, not a deep first-loss decision**: Mark-to-market and call profile can dominate realized return.

#### Monitoring Reminders

1. **Read trustee reports, not just marketing decks**.
2. **Track trend, not just level**: CCC migration, par erosion, and rising special-servicing activity usually show up before formal impairment.
3. **Separate timeless framework from live market inputs**: Use root references for current spreads, issuance context, and benchmark ranges.

Use this framework when analyzing a new ABS, RMBS, CMBS, or WBS offering:

### Pre-Purchase Due Diligence

**1. Deal structure & documentation**
- [ ] Read prospectus, indenture, loan purchase agreement (if applicable)
- [ ] Understand waterfall: Where do coupons, principal, excess spread go?
- [ ] Identify triggers: What events cause early amortization, rapid amortization, special servicing?
- [ ] Review covenants: Are there ongoing performance tests? What are thresholds?
- [ ] Servicer provisions: Who services? Backup servicer designated? Fees?

**2. Collateral quality (asset class specific)**
- [ ] Loan tapes: Review loan-level data (LTV, FICO, DTI, occupancy, purpose, geography, seasoning)
- [ ] Concentration risk: Top-10 obligor % (for CMBS, WBS), geographic concentration (RMBS, CMBS)
- [ ] Sector concentration (CMBS): Office %, retail %, multifamily %, etc.
- [ ] Credit profile: Average FICO (RMBS), average DSCR (CMBS), average payment rate (credit card)
- [ ] Delinquency ramp: Historical delinquency trend (seasonality, vintage ramp-up profile)

**3. Credit enhancement**
- [ ] Subordination levels: % subordination for each tranche (calculate protection for your target tranche)
- [ ] OC (overcollateralization): Initial OC %, OC ramp-up/decline provisions
- [ ] Excess spread: What's initial excess spread? Does it exceed 100 bps (comfortable cushion)?
- [ ] Reserve account: Size, replenishment mechanism
- [ ] Other: Surety, LOC, yield supplement?

**4. Performance metrics & modeling**
- [ ] Historical loss profile: CNL %, CDR %, severity % for comparable pools/vintages
- [ ] Prepayment modeling: What's base case prepayment speed? Range under rates ±200 bps?
- [ ] WAL sensitivity: Calculate WAL under 50%, 100%, 200% PSA (RMBS) or expected CPR scenarios
- [ ] Stress scenarios: Model 1.5x, 2x, 3x loss stress. Does your tranche still achieve target rating?
- [ ] Breakeven analysis: How much loss does it take to impair your tranche? (Loss absorption cushion)

**5. Relative value**
- [ ] Comparable trades: What are similar ABS / RMBS / CMBS tranches trading at? Spreads?
- [ ] Yield: What's your all-in yield including OAS, discount margin?
- [ ] Carry: If you hold 6 months, what cash flow do you collect? (Carry = forward returns if rates/spreads unchanged)
- [ ] Risk/reward: Is yield sufficient for credit risk, prepayment risk, extension risk you're taking?

**6. Originator / servicer quality**
- [ ] Originator track record: Historical loss performance? Still solvent? Repurchase capability?
- [ ] Servicer track record: Delinquency management, loss mitigation effectiveness, regulatory issues?
- [ ] Backup servicer designated: Who steps in if primary fails?

### Ongoing Monitoring

**Monthly after purchase**:
- [ ] Receive servicer report: Delinquency rates, defaults, prepayments, excess spread, trigger status
- [ ] Check triggers: Are any triggers close to firing? Monitor excess spread, delinquency, loss accumulation
- [ ] Monitor performance vs. expectations: Is CPR, CDR, severity in line with expectations?
- [ ] Track prices: Are similar tranches tightening/widening? Relative value shifted?

**Quarterly**:
- [ ] Portfolio review: What's my allocation to this security? Rebalance if needed
- [ ] Covenant compliance check: Are any DSCR/leverage tests in danger of failing? (WBS)
- [ ] Update loss models: Incorporate latest performance data, update future loss assumptions

**Annually**:
- [ ] Comprehensive review: Compare year-ago metrics to current. Trends?
- [ ] Rebalance: Do I still want this security? Prices moved? Spreads attractive?
- [ ] Document thesis: Articulate the investment case, risks, any assumption changes
