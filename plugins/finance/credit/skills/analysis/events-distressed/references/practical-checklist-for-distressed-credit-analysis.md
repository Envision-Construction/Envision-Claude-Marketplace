## Practical Checklist for Distressed Credit Analysis

### Distressed / Restructuring Situation

- [ ] **Root cause**: Is the problem operational, leverage-driven, legal/structural, or a combination?
- [ ] **Timing pressure**: When does the company hit the cash wall, springing maturity, covenant cliff, or refinancing deadline?
- [ ] **Process path**: What are the realistic resolution paths: amend-and-extend, exchange, LME, prepack, freefall, sale, or liquidation?
- [ ] **Claim mapping**: What is the allowed claim, where does it sit in the entity structure, and what liens or guarantees support it?
- [ ] **Value framework**: Is recovery best analyzed through going-concern EV, collateral value, liquidation value, or a hybrid?
- [ ] **Waterfall**: After administrative claims, DIP effects, fees, and senior classes, where does value break?
- [ ] **Fulcrum and control**: Which class controls the outcome, and what leverage do ad hoc groups or blocking positions create?
- [ ] **Present value**: What is the recovery worth after adjusting for time, illiquidity, and process risk?
- [ ] **Key legal risks**: Could litigation, avoidance actions, equitable subordination, or documentation disputes change recoveries?
- [ ] **Catalysts and invalidation points**: What events would improve, impair, or invalidate the underwriting?

### Event-Driven Credit Situation

- [ ] **Event type**: Is this an acquisition, sale, change of control, IPO, refinancing, sponsor action, or governance event?
- [ ] **Close risk**: What must happen for the event to close, and what happens to creditors if it does not?
- [ ] **Pro forma capital structure**: How do leverage, coverage, liquidity, and debt ranking change if the event closes?
- [ ] **Structural impact**: Are existing creditors being primed, refinanced, assumed, retired, or subordinated?
- [ ] **Documentation check**: Do covenants, baskets, puts, or consent rights change the economic outcome?
- [ ] **Ownership and incentives**: How do sponsor objectives, management incentives, or buyer quality affect downside?
- [ ] **Fallback scenario**: What is the credit if the event is delayed, repriced, or abandoned?
- [ ] **Investor action set**: Is the right posture to own, reduce, hedge, litigate, or wait for a more favorable entry point?
