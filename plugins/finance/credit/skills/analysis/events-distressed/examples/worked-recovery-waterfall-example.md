# Worked Example: Recovery Waterfall Analysis — PremierTech Solutions

## Situation Overview

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Company | PremierTech Solutions, Inc. |
| Sector | Enterprise Software (legacy license + cloud transition) |
| Rating | CCC+ / Caa1 (S&P / Moody's), on negative outlook |
| Revenue | $500M LTM (declining from $550M peak) |
| EBITDA | $85M LTM (declining from $120M peak two years prior) |
| Revenue Mix | 60% recurring (maintenance/SaaS), 40% license/services |
| Distress Driver | Failed cloud transition consuming cash; legacy license revenue declining 15% annually |
| Liquidity | $28M cash + $22M revolver availability = $50M total |
| Monthly Cash Burn | ~$6M (accelerating) |
| Liquidity Runway | ~8 months without intervention |

PremierTech is an enterprise software company that has invested heavily in a cloud platform migration over the past three years. The transition has cannibalized higher-margin license revenue faster than SaaS bookings have ramped. R&D spend remains elevated ($75M/year) to complete the platform. The company faces a liquidity crisis and is exploring strategic alternatives.

## Capital Structure

| Tranche | Outstanding | Rate | Maturity | Security |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Revolving Credit Facility | $30M drawn / $50M committed | SOFR + 350 | Mar 2027 | 1st Lien (pari passu with TL) |
| First Lien Term Loan B | $400M | SOFR + 425 | Sep 2028 | 1st Lien on all assets |
| Second Lien Term Loan | $150M | SOFR + 750 | Sep 2029 | 2nd Lien on all assets |
| Senior Unsecured Notes | $100M | 8.50% fixed | Mar 2030 | Unsecured |
| **Total Funded Debt** | **$680M** | | | |

Total leverage: 8.0x ($680M / $85M). First lien leverage: 5.1x ($430M / $85M).

## Enterprise Valuation

### Methodology
Given PremierTech's distressed state, enterprise value is estimated using a blended approach: (1) EBITDA multiple on stressed earnings and (2) revenue multiple on recurring revenue base.

### EBITDA Multiple Approach

| Scenario | Stress EBITDA | Multiple | Enterprise Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low case | $85M (current run-rate) | 5.0x | $425M |
| Mid case | $85M | 5.75x | $489M |
| High case | $85M | 6.5x | $553M |

Multiple range of 5.0x–6.5x reflects: distressed enterprise software transactions (median 5.5x–6.0x for companies mid-cloud-transition), discount for execution risk on incomplete platform migration, premium for $300M recurring revenue base (60% of total revenue).

### Revenue Multiple Cross-Check (Recurring Revenue Only)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Recurring revenue | $300M |
| Distressed SaaS multiple | 1.5x–2.0x |
| Implied EV range | $450M–$600M |
| Blended with EBITDA approach | Supports mid-to-high case |

## Fulcrum Security Identification

The **First Lien Term Loan** is the fulcrum security across all scenarios:

| Scenario | 1L Fully Covered? | Value Break Point |
|---|---|---|
| Low ($425M) | No — 71% recovery | Value breaks within 1L |
| Mid ($489M) | No — 87% recovery | Value breaks within 1L |
| High ($553M) | Yes — 100% recovery | Value breaks within 2L |

At the mid-case enterprise value, the 1L term loan is impaired by $55M ($412M claim vs. $357M available). The fulcrum shifts to the 2L only in the high case, where 1L is fully covered but only $9M of value flows through to 2L.

### Fulcrum Sensitivity

| EBITDA Multiple | Enterprise Value | 1L Recovery | Fulcrum Tranche |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.5x | $383M | 61% | 1L Term Loan |
| 5.0x | $425M | 71% | 1L Term Loan |
| 5.75x | $489M | 87% | 1L Term Loan |
| 6.5x | $553M | 100% | 2L Term Loan |
| 7.0x | $595M | 100% | 2L Term Loan |

The fulcrum shifts from 1L to 2L at approximately 6.2x EBITDA ($527M EV), the breakeven multiple where 1L claims ($412M) are fully covered after administrative claims ($25M), DIP ($75M), and revolver ($32M) totaling $132M are satisfied.

## DIP Impact Analysis

### Impact on Pre-Petition 1L Recovery

| Metric | Without DIP | With $75M DIP |
|---|---|---|
| 1L recovery at mid-case | 95%* | 87% |
| Recovery reduction | — | -8 points |
| Dollar impact ($412M claim) | — | -$33M less available to 1L |

*Without DIP: $489M - $25M admin = $464M available for $444M 1L claims (revolver + TL), yielding ~100% for revolver and 95% for TL.

### DIP Justification
Despite the 8-point recovery dilution at the mid-case, the DIP is value-accretive for the estate:
- Without DIP, the company liquidates within 8 months. Liquidation value of software assets (IP, customer contracts, brand) estimated at $200M–$250M, yielding 1L recovery of only 40%–50%.
- DIP-funded restructuring preserves going-concern value ($489M mid-case vs. $225M liquidation midpoint), increasing 1L recovery by ~37 points.

## Pro Forma Capitalization (Post-Restructuring)

Assuming mid-case emergence at $489M enterprise value:

| Component | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Exit revolver | $50M (undrawn) | New facility |
| Exit first lien term loan | $200M | Take-back debt from existing 1L lenders |
| Pro forma equity | $257M | Distributed to 1L lenders (for $55M impairment) |
| **Total Enterprise Value** | **$489M** | 2.4x exit leverage on $85M EBITDA |

1L lenders holding $412M in claims receive: $357M in take-back debt (87% par recovery in cash/new debt) plus equity stake representing the $55M shortfall. If the reorganized entity appreciates from the exit valuation, 1L lenders capture upside through their equity position.
