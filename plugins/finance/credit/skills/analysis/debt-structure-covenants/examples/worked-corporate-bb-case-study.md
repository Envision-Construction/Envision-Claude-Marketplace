### Covenant Analysis

**Financial Maintenance Covenant (TLB only):**
- Max Net Leverage: 6.5x (step-down to 6.0x in Year 3)
- Springing Test: Only tested if revolver drawn >35% ($35M)

**Current Headroom:**
- Actual Net Leverage: 5.2x vs. 6.5x limit = 20% headroom
- Analyst EBITDA basis: 5.2x vs. 6.5x = 20% headroom (comfortable)
- But: step-down to 6.0x in 18 months reduces headroom to 13%

**Silent Debt Capacity Assessment:**

| Basket | Capacity | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Ratio Debt (≤ 4.0x Secured) | $0 | Already at 4.4x secured — basket exhausted |
| Builder Basket | $85M | 50% of cumulative CNI since closing ($170M × 50%) |
| General Basket | $50M | Fixed dollar carve-out |
| Incremental Facility (MFN) | $100M | Subject to 2.0x FCCR incurrence test (currently 1.9x — marginally available) |
| Contribution Debt | Unlimited | $ for $ with equity contributions |
| **Total Silent Capacity** | **$235M** | **0.9x turns of additional leverage** |

**Risk Assessment:** $235M of silent capacity represents 0.9x additional leverage. If fully drawn, total leverage would reach 6.4x — within covenant but leaving zero headroom. The builder basket ($85M) is the highest-risk component as it requires no incurrence test and can fund restricted payments (dividends to sponsor).

**Documentation Red Flags:**

| Flag | Severity | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Uncapped synergy add-backs | HIGH | No cap on projected synergies in EBITDA definition — inflates covenant EBITDA above analyst EBITDA |
| Builder basket funds RP | MODERATE | Basket can fund restricted payments without leverage test gate |
| Broad "Permitted Holders" | MODERATE | Change of control not triggered by sponsor-to-sponsor secondary sale |
| No anti-LME provisions | HIGH | No uptier protection, no anti-Serta language, no sacred rights protection for non-pro-rata treatment |
| 30% EBITDA addback cap | LOW | Cap exists but 30% is generous; current addbacks at 8% |

**LME Vulnerability Score: 6/10 (Moderate-High)**
- Missing anti-Serta provisions (+2)
- Missing sacred rights protection (+2)
- Silent capacity >0.5x (+1)
- Builder basket funds RP (+1)
- EBITDA addback cap exists (-1)
- No unrestricted sub history (-1)
