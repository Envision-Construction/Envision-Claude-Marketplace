# Third-Party Report Assessment for Credit Due Diligence

## Critical Reading Principle

Every third-party report in a data room was commissioned to answer a specific question for a party with a specific objective. The first task is not to accept or reject the report. It is to identify whose question it was designed to answer, what scope the provider was asked to cover, and what important credit questions were left outside that scope.

This does not make third-party reports useless. It makes them evidence with boundaries.

## Universal Questions for Any Report

Before using any third-party report in underwriting, answer:

1. **Who commissioned it?** Seller, sponsor, borrower, lender, buyer, or counsel.
2. **What was the scope?** Full review, limited procedures, desktop review, selected sites, selected contracts, or management-provided data only.
3. **What was not independently verified?** Management representations, unaudited schedules, projections, or legal assumptions.
4. **What is the stale-date risk?** Has the business, asset base, or regulatory setting changed since the report was prepared?
5. **What is the credit transmission mechanism?** Cash cost, liquidity drain, collateral impairment, covenant pressure, operational disruption, or refinancing friction.

If a report cannot be tied to one of those transmission mechanisms, it may be interesting but not decision-critical.

## What Good Use Looks Like

### QoE Reports

- Use the QoE as a challenge tool, not as an automatic replacement for judgment.
- Focus on the bridge from reported results to adjusted EBITDA, the working-capital normalization logic, and the evidence behind any forward-looking or run-rate claim.
- If the QoE relies heavily on management representations, partial procedures, or limited sample testing, confidence should be lower.
- For detailed EBITDA adjustment discipline, provider quality, and model translation, use `references/qoe-report-interpretation.md`.

### Legal Diligence

Read legal work through four questions:

1. **How large could the liability be?**
2. **When could it crystallize?**
3. **Would insurance, indemnity, or contract rights offset it?**
4. **Could it impair operations before it impairs cash?**

Priority issues include:
- Material litigation or investigations without a credible reserve or defense path
- Change-of-control or assignment restrictions in key contracts
- Permits, licenses, or regulatory approvals that are essential to operations
- IP ownership or licensing gaps where the asset or service depends on protected rights

The legal report becomes credit-relevant when it changes expected cash taxes, legal spend, reserve needs, covenant headroom, or go-forward operating rights.

### Environmental Reports

Start by distinguishing what the report actually proves:

- **Phase I** identifies concerns and history; it does not quantify contamination.
- **Phase II** tests whether contamination exists and begins to size the issue.
- **Remediation workplan** translates contamination into money, timing, and operational restriction.

Key questions:
- Is there an unresolved Recognized Environmental Condition or similar flagged concern?
- Is a sampling follow-up required but not yet complete?
- Does any contamination risk sit on a material property or operating asset?
- Is there a realistic remediation estimate and a party clearly responsible for it?
- Are there operating restrictions, monitoring obligations, or control requirements that survive closing?

Environmental diligence matters most when it creates unbounded liability, collateral impairment, trapped cash, delayed exit, or refinancing friction.

### Insurance Reviews

Insurance is rarely about whether coverage exists. It is about whether coverage is adequate for the loss scenario that matters.

Check:
- Limits versus a plausible severe-but-realistic loss
- Deductibles and retentions versus borrower liquidity
- Named exclusions and sub-limits that carve out the exact risk you care about
- Business interruption scope, indemnity period, and whether contingent suppliers or customers are covered

A company can appear insured and still have a material uncovered exposure because the sub-limit, waiting period, exclusion, or retention is misaligned with the real risk.

### Tax Diligence

Tax work becomes credit-relevant when it changes cash taxes, reduces expected deductions, or introduces contingent claims.

Watch for:
- Structure choices that alter post-close cash tax burden
- NOLs or other tax assets that look valuable but are constrained by ownership change or usage limits
- Aggressive transfer pricing or uncertain positions that could trigger audit payments, penalties, or trapped cash
- Jurisdictional complexity that raises recurring compliance cost or dispute risk

The question is not whether the structure is elegant. It is whether the underwriting case assumes tax benefits that are actually available and sustainable.

### Market Studies

Third-party market studies are best used as boundary checks, not as the sole basis for a growth thesis.

Review:
- Who defined the market and how narrow that definition is
- Whether the study relies on primary work, public data, or management-fed assumptions
- Whether the market growth narrative is consistent with the company's actual growth and competitive position
- Whether downside cases, substitution risk, and competitive intensity are meaningfully addressed

For detailed de-biasing of CIMs and growth narratives, use `references/sell-side-bias-assessment.md`.

## Translating Reports into Credit Work

Every report should produce one or more of the following outputs:

- **Adjustment**: Change a revenue, EBITDA, working-capital, capex, reserve, or cash-tax assumption
- **Condition**: Require a deliverable, remediation plan, covenant, reserve, or holdback before closing
- **Escalation**: Hand the issue to documentation, modeling, or committee review because it changes downside protection
- **Disclosure**: State clearly in the memo that a risk remains unresolved and how that uncertainty affects conviction

If a report identifies an issue but no underwriting output changes, re-check whether the issue is truly material.
