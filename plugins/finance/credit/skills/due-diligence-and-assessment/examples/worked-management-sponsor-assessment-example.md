# Worked Example: Management & Sponsor Assessment — HealthBridge Diagnostics

## Company Profile

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Company | HealthBridge Diagnostics, Inc. |
| Sector | Healthcare — Diagnostics & Life Sciences Tools |
| Revenue | $350M LTM |
| EBITDA | $67M LTM (19.1% margin) |
| Total Leverage | 5.2x |
| Rating | B+ / B1 |
| Sponsor | Pinnacle Capital Partners (Fund IV) |
| Acquisition Date | July 2024 |
| Acquisition Multiple | 12.5x EBITDA (pre-synergy) |
| Equity Check | $270M (40% of TEV) |

HealthBridge provides diagnostic testing equipment, consumables, and software to hospital laboratories and reference labs across the US. Revenue split: 55% consumables (recurring), 30% equipment, 15% software/services. The company was acquired by Pinnacle Capital Partners in a take-private transaction.

## Management Assessment

### CEO — Dr. Sarah Chen

| Dimension | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Tenure | 8 years (pre-LBO), promoted from COO |
| Industry Experience | 20 years in healthcare/diagnostics |
| Prior Leadership | VP Operations at MedLab Corp (5 years) |
| LBO Experience | **None** — never managed a company with leverage >3.5x; current leverage is 5.2x |
| Capital Allocation Track Record | Conservative historically; 2 bolt-on acquisitions (well-integrated) |
| Key Person Risk | High — deep customer relationships, institutional knowledge |
| Alignment | Rolled over $8M equity (3% of sponsor equity check) |

**Positives**: Deep domain expertise, operational continuity through LBO transition, strong customer relationships. Bolt-on acquisition track record suggests disciplined M&A approach.

**Concerns**: Untested at current leverage levels. Prior experience was at an investment-grade company with conservative capital structure. May not be prepared for the cash management discipline and covenant navigation required at 5.2x leverage.

### CFO — Michael Torres

| Dimension | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Tenure | 14 months (hired post-LBO by sponsor, September 2024) |
| Prior Roles | CFO at 3 PE-backed companies (healthcare services, med devices, pharma distribution) |
| LBO Experience | Extensive — managed leverage of 4.0x–6.5x across prior roles |
| Shortest Prior Tenure | 18 months (pharma distribution — departed after strategic disagreement with sponsor) |
| Longest Prior Tenure | 3.5 years (med devices — successful exit at 2.3x MOIC) |
| Alignment | $1.5M co-invest + standard option package |

**Positives**: Highly relevant experience managing PE-backed healthcare companies through leveraged capital structures. Has navigated covenant amendments and refinancings in prior roles.

**Concerns**: Flight risk — shortest tenure was 18 months, and the pattern suggests he moves on after initial post-LBO stabilization. If Torres departs within 24 months, CFO transition during a critical deleveraging period would be disruptive. Hired by sponsor (not CEO-selected), suggesting potential principal-agent tension.

### COO — James Whitfield

| Dimension | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Tenure | 4 months (hired January 2026) |
| Prior Role | SVP Operations at Arcadian Health Systems |
| Industry Experience | 12 years in healthcare operations |
| LBO Experience | None |

**Concerns**: Too new to evaluate operational impact. Critical role given need to drive $12M identified cost synergies. Departure of predecessor COO (who left 2 months after LBO close) is itself a red flag — raises questions about cultural fit with sponsor-driven operational changes.

## Sponsor Assessment — Pinnacle Capital Partners

### Fund Profile

| Parameter | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fund | Fund IV ($1.8B, 2022 vintage) |
| Fund Deployment | 65% deployed (12 portfolio companies) |
| Remaining Dry Powder | ~$630M |
| Fund Life | Year 4 of 10 |
| GP Commitment | 3% ($54M) |
| Healthcare Allocation | 4 deals (33% of fund) |
| Firm AUM | $6.5B across 5 funds |

### Track Record — Healthcare Deals

| Deal | Vintage | Entry Multiple | Current Status | MOIC | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clearview Labs | 2019 (Fund III) | 11.0x | Exited 2023 | 2.1x | Successful exit via strategic sale; executed bolt-on strategy well |
| Meridian Health Services | 2021 (Fund III) | 13.0x | Current | ~1.4x (est.) | Performing; EBITDA growth on track; refinanced at lower spread |
| CarePoint Pharmacy | 2022 (Fund IV) | 10.5x | Restructured | 0.6x | Covenant breach at 18 months; sponsor delayed equity cure by 45 days, causing unnecessary credit stress; lender-forced amendment with 150bps fee |
| HealthBridge (this deal) | 2024 (Fund IV) | 12.5x | Current | 1.0x | Early stage; deleveraging thesis in progress |

### Sponsor Behavior Pattern Analysis

| Behavior Dimension | Pattern Observed | Risk Level |
|---|---|---|
| Dividend Recaps | Executed within 14 months of acquisition in 2 of 3 prior deals (Clearview: month 12; CarePoint: month 14) | **High** |
| Follow-on Acquisitions | Aggressive bolt-on strategy in all deals; 3–5 acquisitions per platform | Medium |
| Equity Cure Willingness | Deployed once (CarePoint) but delayed 45 days, creating unnecessary lender anxiety | **Elevated** |
| Management Changes | Installed new CFO in all deals; COO change in 2 of 3 | Medium |
| Fund Vintage Behavior | Fund IV at 65% deployed in Year 4; GP focus likely shifting to deployment over portfolio support | **Elevated** |
| Exit Timeline | Average hold 4–5 years; no premature exits | Low |

### Sponsor Risk Assessment: Moderate-High

**Key concerns**:
1. **Extractive tendencies** — Consistent pattern of early dividend recaps (within 14 months) signals prioritization of returning capital over deleveraging. For HealthBridge at 5.2x leverage, a dividend recap would increase leverage at a time when the credit thesis depends on de-leveraging.
2. **Inconsistent support** — CarePoint episode demonstrates willingness to deploy equity cures but with delays that erode lender confidence. In a liquidity stress scenario, lenders cannot assume timely sponsor support.
3. **Deployment vs. support trade-off** — With $630M of dry powder and Fund IV in Year 4, GP incentives favor deploying capital into new deals rather than supporting existing portfolio companies. HealthBridge may receive less attention than earlier Fund IV investments.

## Handoff to Debt Structure & Covenants

Based on this assessment, the following enhanced documentation review items are flagged for `debt-structure-covenants`:

1. **Restricted payment basket**: Scrutinize for capacity enabling dividend recap within 18 months, given sponsor's consistent pattern. Look for builder basket based on 50% of CNI, available amount baskets, and any general RP capacity exceeding $10M.
2. **Equity cure provisions**: Evaluate cure mechanics — is there a cap on number of cures? Minimum hold period? Can cured EBITDA be used for other covenant calculations? Given Pinnacle's delayed cure at CarePoint, ensure cure must be deployed within 10 business days of compliance certificate delivery.
3. **Management fee provisions**: Verify Pinnacle's $2M annual fee subordination and whether it can be increased. Check for catch-up provisions on deferred fees.
4. **Change-of-control definition**: Confirm sponsor transfer restrictions and whether Fund IV can transfer to Fund V without triggering COC.
5. **Addback definitions**: Given EBITDA-linked management compensation, review permitted addback categories and any caps on run-rate synergies and cost savings.
