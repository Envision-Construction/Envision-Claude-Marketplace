## Documentation Risk Quick-Reference Checklist

Systematic checklist for reading credit agreements. Work through each section when reviewing a new credit agreement or indenture.

### Step 1: EBITDA Definition (Section 1.01 or Definitions)
- [ ] Identify all permitted addbacks beyond standard (restructuring, SBC, transaction costs)
- [ ] Check for synergy/cost-saving addback cap (if uncapped → HIGH RISK)
- [ ] Verify run-rate period for pro forma adjustments (>24 months → elevated risk)
- [ ] Look for "as determined in good faith by the borrower" language (→ subjective, hard to challenge)
- [ ] Calculate: What % of reported EBITDA comes from addbacks? (>25% → RED FLAG)

### Step 2: Debt Incurrence (Section 6.01 / Negative Covenants)
- [ ] Map ALL baskets that permit additional debt (ratio, free-and-clear, incremental, general)
- [ ] Calculate total silent debt capacity: sum of all non-ratio baskets + ratio basket headroom
- [ ] Check if baskets are "use it or lose it" or cumulative
- [ ] Look for reclassification rights (can debt incurred under one basket be redesignated?)
- [ ] Verify MFN (Most Favored Nation) provisions on incremental term loans — sunset period?

### Step 3: Restricted Payments (Section 6.06)
- [ ] Identify builder basket formula and starting balance
- [ ] Check Available Amount definition (CNI-based? Retained Excess Cash Flow?)
- [ ] Calculate maximum leakage under RP baskets at closing leverage
- [ ] Look for "Designated Unrestricted Subsidiary" provisions (J.Crew trapdoor)
- [ ] Verify if dividends require compliance with financial covenant or just ratio test

### Step 4: Investment Baskets (Section 6.04)
- [ ] Total permitted investment capacity at closing
- [ ] Can investments be made in unrestricted subsidiaries? (asset dropout risk)
- [ ] Look for contribution debt mechanics (equity contributions creating debt capacity)
- [ ] Check if investment basket includes "loan to" provisions (value transfer risk)

### Step 5: Asset Sale Provisions (Section 6.05)
- [ ] Mandatory prepayment from asset sale proceeds? What percentage? Stepdowns?
- [ ] Reinvestment rights — how long? 365 days + 180-day extension = 545 days of retained proceeds
- [ ] Fair market value determination — who decides? ("determined in good faith by the borrower")
- [ ] Collateral release provisions — automatic release upon permitted sale?

### Step 6: Structural Protections (Various Sections)
- [ ] Anti-uptier: Does subordination require unanimous consent?
- [ ] Anti-dropdown: Cap on asset transfers to unrestricted subs (<5%?)
- [ ] Non-pro-rata exchange block?
- [ ] Sacred rights adequately defined? (principal, interest, maturity, pro rata sharing, waterfall)
- [ ] Minimum consent threshold for amendments (simple majority? 66.67%? higher?)

### Step 7: Financial Covenant (Section 7.01, if any)
- [ ] Maintenance vs. incurrence-only (cov-lite = incurrence only → no early warning)
- [ ] Springing financial covenant? (drawn >35% of revolver)
- [ ] Equity cure rights? (how many times? consecutive? minimum cure amount?)
- [ ] EBITDA definition for covenant compliance — same as cash flow definition?

### Step 8: Events of Default (Section 8.01)
- [ ] Cross-default thresholds (what $ amount triggers cross-default?)
- [ ] Change of control definition (is a secondary buyout a CoC?)
- [ ] Material adverse change clause — how is "material" defined?
- [ ] Grace/cure periods for non-payment defaults

### Step 9: Intercreditor (Separate Agreement)
- [ ] Payment subordination — hard block or soft (time-limited)?
- [ ] Standstill period for junior creditors (90-180 days typical)
- [ ] Purchase option — can junior creditors buy senior debt at par?
- [ ] DIP financing consent — must junior creditors consent to DIP?
- [ ] Release provisions — does release of senior liens automatically release junior?

### Red Flag Summary
After completing the checklist, tally red flags:
- **0-2 flags**: Standard documentation — proceed with normal analysis
- **3-5 flags**: Elevated documentation risk — flag in memo, quantify silent debt capacity
- **6+ flags**: Material documentation risk — consider spread adjustment of 25-75 bps or decline
