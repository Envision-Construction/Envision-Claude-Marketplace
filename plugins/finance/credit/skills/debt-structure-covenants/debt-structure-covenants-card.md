## Description: <br>
Use when analyzing a credit agreement, bond indenture, intercreditor agreement, amendment package, or capital structure — covering debt ranking, covenant mechanics, documentation leakage, silent debt capacity, LME vulnerability, refinancing structure, and amendment risk for corporate and private-credit borrowers, with a narrow CRE bridge for loan terms and cash-management structures. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
