## The Assignment Agreement — Rights, Consents, and Mechanics

### What an Assignment Is
An **assignment** transfers the seller's lender position under the credit agreement to the buyer. Upon effectiveness, the buyer becomes lender of record and acquires the direct rights that matter for amendments, consents, and enforcement.

### The LSTA Standard Assignment and Assumption
Most broadly syndicated facilities document assignments through a standard form that:

- Identifies the exact facility and amount being transferred.
- Brings the buyer into the credit agreement without rewriting the facility terms.
- Requires the transfer conditions in the credit agreement to be satisfied.
- Allocates fees, tax forms, and closing mechanics between the parties.

### Consent Requirements — Three-Party Consent
Consent mechanics vary by document, but the recurring questions are:

1. Does the borrower have approval or objection rights?
2. Does the administrative agent need to countersign or process the transfer?
3. Is the proposed buyer disqualified, non-eligible, or otherwise blocked by the agreement?
4. Are there any additional lender-consent provisions for control or concentrated transfers?

Never rely on market habit alone; the credit agreement governs.

### Minimum Assignment Amounts
Minimum hold or minimum assignment provisions exist to avoid fragmented lender lists. Confirm the operative threshold in the credit agreement and the current market norm in root `references/typical-deal-parameters.md`.

### Assignment Fees
Assignments often involve agent processing fees. The amount and payer are negotiable economics, not timeless doctrine, so confirm the current convention in root references and the specific deal documents.

### Tax Documentation
The buyer typically must deliver the tax and status forms required under the credit agreement and agent process. Missing or incorrect forms can delay settlement or alter withholding economics.
