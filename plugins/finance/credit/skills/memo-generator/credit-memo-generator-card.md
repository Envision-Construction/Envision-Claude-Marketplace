## Description: <br>
Orchestration skill that produces structured Investment Committee (IC) credit memos, guiding the analyst through a complete credit analysis workflow that pulls from other skills to assemble a comprehensive memo covering business overview, financial analysis, capital structure, relative value, risks, and recommendation across corporate (IG/HY), private credit, CRE, and structured finance credit types. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>
