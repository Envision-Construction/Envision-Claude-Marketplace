## Comprehensive Glossary of Key Terms

Use this file as a quick scan for recurring terminology. For deeper treatment, load the more specific reference named in `SKILL.md`.

### Pricing and Return

**Basis Point (bp)**
- One hundredth of one percent.
- Standard unit for quoting spreads, fees, and yield changes.

**Par**
- Face value or principal amount of a debt instrument.

**Premium / Discount**
- Price above par / below par.

**OID (Original Issue Discount)**
- Purchase discount at issuance that boosts investor yield.

**Coupon**
- Contractual interest rate on a bond.

**Spread / Applicable Margin**
- Credit spread over the benchmark base rate or curve.

**YTM / YTW / OAS**
- Common shorthand for bond yield-to-maturity, yield-to-worst, and option-adjusted spread.

**Discount Margin**
- Spread-style measure used for floating-rate instruments.

### Capital Structure

**Senior Secured**
- Higher-priority claim backed by collateral.

**Senior Unsecured**
- Ranks behind secured debt but ahead of subordinated obligations.

**Subordinated Debt**
- Debt contractually or structurally junior to more senior claims.

**Pari Passu**
- Ranking equally with another claim.

**Structural Subordination**
- Creditors at a holding company sit behind creditors of operating subsidiaries with direct claims on assets and cash flow.

**Mezzanine Debt**
- Junior capital sitting between senior debt and equity, often with higher yield or equity-like features.

### Loan and Bond Instruments

**Revolver**
- Committed line that can be drawn, repaid, and redrawn.

**Term Loan**
- Funded loan with a stated maturity and repayment profile.

**TLA / TLB**
- Bank-oriented / institutional-oriented forms of syndicated term loans.

**PIK (Payment-in-Kind)**
- Interest paid by issuing additional debt instead of cash.

**Toggle**
- Option to switch between cash-pay and PIK interest, subject to deal terms.

**Make-Whole Call**
- Redemption formula designed to compensate bondholders for foregone coupon economics.

### Documentation and Control

**Covenant**
- Contractual requirement or restriction on the borrower.

**Maintenance Covenant**
- Ongoing financial test measured regularly.

**Incurrence Covenant**
- Test triggered only when the borrower takes a specified action.

**Credit Agreement**
- Governing legal document for a loan.

**Indenture**
- Governing legal document for a bond.

**Prospectus / Offering Memorandum**
- Disclosure and marketing document describing the issuer, securities, and risks.

**Sacred Rights**
- Amendments that generally require all or nearly all affected lenders or bondholders to consent.

### Syndication and Secondary Market

**Lead Arranger / Bookrunner**
- Institution leading structuring, marketing, and allocation of a financing.

**Administrative Agent**
- Party administering payment flows, notices, and lender records after closing.

**Assignment**
- Transfer of a loan position with lender-of-record status moving to the buyer.

**Participation**
- Transfer of economic exposure while lender-of-record status stays with the seller.

**Market Flex**
- Right to change pricing or structure during syndication if demand requires it.

### Distressed and Liability Management Bridge Terms

**Fulcrum Security**
- The part of the capital structure most likely to control the restructuring outcome because it sits at the recovery boundary.

**DIP Financing**
- Post-petition financing with enhanced priority in bankruptcy.

**Uptier / Priming**
- Transaction that moves participating creditors into a more senior position.

**Dropdown**
- Transfer of assets outside the restricted credit group, often weakening creditor support value.
