# Credit Monitoring Framework

## Philosophy: Monitoring Is Not Re-Underwriting

The purpose of surveillance is to test whether the original investment thesis still holds. Monitoring should begin with the assumptions that mattered at entry, convert them into observable signals, and focus analyst time where the thesis is drifting, not where the name is simply producing routine data.

Effective surveillance is therefore triage-based:
- low-intensity review for credits performing as expected
- heightened attention for names showing early thesis drift
- decision-oriented monitoring for names approaching structural stress, forced action, or workout

Current monitoring cadences and operational targets should come from `references/data-pipeline-specification.md` rather than being embedded in this framework.

## What to Monitor

### Financial Performance Versus the Underwriting Case
- revenue, EBITDA, margin, and free cash flow relative to the model
- whether misses are temporary, cyclical, or structural
- working-capital behavior and cash conversion quality
- differences between reported, adjusted, and covenant-defined metrics

### Liquidity and Capital Structure
- cash availability and access to revolver or committed facilities
- debt maturity profile and realistic refinancing options
- covenant headroom and forward compliance under base and stress cases
- amendment, waiver, and refinancing history as signals of rising fragility

### Market and External Signals
- price, spread, and CDS behavior where available
- dealer mark dispersion and liquidity quality
- rating actions and watch placements
- peer or sector-level stress that may either confirm or contextualize the name-specific move

### Qualitative and Event Risk
- management credibility, turnover, and governance quality
- sponsor behavior and alignment
- M&A, litigation, regulation, and commercial developments
- deterioration in reporting quality, timeliness, or transparency

## Interpreting Deterioration

- Look for confirmation across multiple dimensions rather than reacting to one metric in isolation.
- Distinguish technical pressure from fundamental change.
- Focus on the path, not just the point-in-time level: accelerating deterioration matters more than a weak but stable metric.
- Treat structural or documentation-related risk as equal in importance to earnings deterioration.

## Portfolio-Level Surveillance

Single-name monitoring should roll into portfolio oversight. The portfolio lens should answer:
- where concentrations are building through active decision or passive drift
- whether downgrades, watchlist additions, or maturities are clustering by sector, sponsor, or vintage
- whether a forced action in one stressed position creates second-order portfolio problems elsewhere
- whether correlations revealed by deterioration are larger than originally assumed

Use `references/portfolio-risk-parameters.md` for current limits and `references/portfolio-level-ic-review.md` for portfolio review structure.

## Integration with Trading and Governance

Surveillance is incomplete if it stops at description. Each monitoring cycle should answer:
- is the thesis intact, weakened, or broken
- what could happen next if current trends continue
- what action set is now live: hold, add, reduce, hedge, exit, or engage
- who needs to know now: analyst only, PM, full IC, or distressed/workout stakeholders

Escalation ownership and required documentation belong in `references/escalation-framework.md`, with current thresholds in `references/escalation-trigger-thresholds.md`.

## Asset-Class Overlay

The surveillance framework is common across asset classes, but the relevant indicators differ:
- corporate credit emphasizes earnings durability, leverage path, and refinancing access
- private credit adds maintenance covenants, sponsor behavior, and information-rights quality
- CRE emphasizes property cash flow, tenancy, collateral value, and refinanceability
- structured finance emphasizes collateral migration, trigger-test cushion, and manager or servicer performance

Use the instrument-specific references for these overlays rather than trying to force one generic metric set across all structures.
