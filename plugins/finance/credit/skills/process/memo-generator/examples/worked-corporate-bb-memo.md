## IC Memo: MedTech Holdings, Inc. — Term Loan B (SOFR+400, 2030)

This is a **synthetic teaching example** of a BB corporate IC memo. It is meant to demonstrate memo structure, synthesis, and disclosure standards rather than provide live market data or a real issuer recommendation. ### Executive Summary
**Recommendation:** PASS (Conditional) | **Conviction:** Medium | **Position Size:** 1.5% ($15M of $1B portfolio)

MedTech Holdings is a BB-/B+ healthcare staffing company with $250M analyst EBITDA and 5.5x total leverage. The credit offers adequate current yield (SOFR+400) for a B+/BB- risk profile but trades at the tight end of fair value vs. peers (12bps wide of cohort median, warranted by documentation risk). We recommend a conditional pass subject to SOFR hedging confirmation and anti-LME amendment language.

**Thesis-Kill Triggers:** (1) Loss of any top-5 customer (35% concentration), (2) EBITDA decline >15% triggering covenant breach, (3) Sponsor dividend recap pre-deleveraging.

### Business Overview
*Source: industry-sector-analysis (healthcare services sub-sector)*

MedTech Holdings operates in the physician staffing and outsourced healthcare services segment, providing temporary and permanent staffing solutions to hospital systems across 28 states. The company serves approximately 200 healthcare facilities, with revenue driven by staffing hours billed at negotiated rates.

**Sector Dynamics:** Healthcare staffing is a fragmented, labor-intensive market with structural tailwinds (aging population, physician shortages) offset by cyclical risks (reimbursement rate changes, labor supply constraints). The sector exhibits moderate operating leverage — fixed costs (recruiting infrastructure, compliance, technology platform) represent approximately 40% of the cost base.

**Competitive Position:** MedTech holds ~3% market share in a $50B+ addressable market. Key differentiators include a proprietary credentialing platform, multi-state licensure network, and long-term MSA relationships with hospital systems. Barriers to entry are moderate (regulatory compliance, credentialing requirements).

**Key Risk — Customer Concentration:** Top 5 hospital systems represent 35% of revenue. Loss of any single top-5 client would result in 5-8% revenue decline with limited near-term replacement capacity due to MSA procurement cycles (12-18 months for new large contracts).

**Key Risk — Labor Supply:** Nurse and physician supply constraints drive wage inflation of 3-5% annually, compressing margins if contract rate escalators lag. Post-COVID staffing dynamics have stabilized but remain a structural headwind.

**Key Risk — Reimbursement:** Medicaid-heavy states (30% of revenue) face potential rate adjustments under budget pressure. Medicare reimbursement changes could affect hospital system clients' willingness to maintain staffing budgets.

### Financial Analysis
*Source: modeling-and-valuation (see worked case study)*

- **Analyst EBITDA:** $250M (vs. management $270M — $20M haircut for recurring "integration" costs)
- **Leverage:** 5.5x total / 4.4x secured (B+ territory)
- **Coverage:** 2.8x (tight; compresses to 2.2x at +200bps SOFR)
- **FCF:** $85M (6.2% FCF/Debt — adequate deleveraging capacity)
- **Downside:** 15% revenue decline produces 7.2x leverage, covenant breach within 2 quarters
- **Rate Sensitivity:** 200bps SOFR cushion before covenant breach; no hedging in place

### Capital Structure & Covenants
*Source: debt-structure-covenants (see worked case study)*

- **Silent Capacity:** $235M (0.9x additional turns) — moderate risk
- **LME Vulnerability:** 6/10 — no anti-Serta, no sacred rights protection
- **Covenant Headroom:** 18% on 6.5x max leverage (step-down to 6.0x in 18 months reduces to 13%)
- **Key Documentation Risk:** Uncapped synergy addbacks, builder basket funds restricted payments without leverage gate

### Relative Value
*Source: trading-pricing-mechanics (see worked case study)*

- **Spread:** SOFR+400 vs. cohort median SOFR+388 (+12bps wide)
- **Warranted Range:** SOFR+400-415 (leverage + documentation risk adjustment)
- **Verdict:** Fair to marginally tight — not a compelling entry point
- **Preferred Entry:** Wait for SOFR+425+ or documentation improvement (anti-LME amendment)

### Risk Assessment

| Risk | Probability | Severity | Thesis Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer concentration (top-5 = 35%) | Medium | High | Loss of 1 major client = 7%+ revenue decline, thesis under pressure |
| SOFR increase +200bps | Medium | High | Coverage drops to 2.2x, covenant headroom to 5% |
| Sponsor dividend recap | Medium | Medium | Increases leverage 0.5-1.0x via builder basket, delays deleveraging |

### Recommendation
- **Action:** Conditional Pass at 1.5% position ($15M notional)
- **Conditions:** (1) Confirm SOFR hedging strategy within 30 days, (2) Legal review of anti-LME amendment feasibility
- **Monitoring Cadence:** Quarterly | First review: next earnings release
- **Entry Parameters:** Only at SOFR+425+ (current +400 is at tight end of fair value)
