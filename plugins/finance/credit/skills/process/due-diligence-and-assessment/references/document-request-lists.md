# Standard Document Request Lists by Deal Type

## LBO / New Acquisition

### Financial Documents
- [ ] Audited financial statements — 3 most recent fiscal years (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement, notes)
- [ ] Quarterly or monthly management accounts — trailing 18-24 months
- [ ] Current year budget and multi-year financial projections (management case)
- [ ] Quality of Earnings (QoE) report — buy-side and/or sell-side
- [ ] Working capital analysis — normalized working capital, seasonality, component detail
- [ ] Capex schedule — maintenance vs growth breakdown, forward capex plan, major project detail
- [ ] Debt schedule — all outstanding indebtedness, terms, maturity dates, interest rates
- [ ] Tax returns — 3 most recent fiscal years (federal and material state/local)
- [ ] Accounts receivable aging — current and 2 prior quarter-ends
- [ ] Inventory analysis — aging, obsolescence reserves, turnover by category
- [ ] Accounts payable aging — current and 2 prior quarter-ends
- [ ] Revenue detail — by customer (top 20 minimum), by product/service, by geography
- [ ] EBITDA addback detail — supporting documentation for each material adjustment
- [ ] Cash flow bridge — EBITDA to free cash flow with all components identified
- [ ] Intercompany transactions — nature, amounts, and elimination in consolidation

### Business Documents
- [ ] Confidential Information Memorandum (CIM) — arranger's marketing document
- [ ] Management presentation — company-prepared business overview and strategy
- [ ] Customer analysis — top 20 customers with revenue, tenure, contract terms, renewal dates
- [ ] Customer contracts — material customer agreements (top 5-10 by revenue)
- [ ] Vendor analysis — top 10 suppliers with spend, relationship tenure, concentration risk
- [ ] Vendor contracts — material supply agreements
- [ ] Market study — independent industry analysis (if available)
- [ ] Competitive landscape — market share data, key competitors, barriers to entry
- [ ] Organizational chart — corporate structure and key subsidiaries
- [ ] Key employee list — senior management bios, tenure, compensation summary
- [ ] Technology and IT systems — overview of key systems, cybersecurity assessment
- [ ] Sales pipeline — current pipeline data and win rates (for B2B businesses)
- [ ] Backlog analysis — for project-based or manufacturing businesses

### Legal Documents
- [ ] Draft or executed credit agreement — including all schedules and exhibits
- [ ] Intercreditor agreement (if applicable) — priority, subordination, waterfall
- [ ] Security agreement — collateral scope, excluded assets, perfection requirements
- [ ] Guaranty agreement — guarantor coverage, excluded subsidiaries
- [ ] Material contracts — any contract representing >5% of revenue or critical to operations
- [ ] IP portfolio — patents, trademarks, trade secrets, licenses
- [ ] Litigation summary — all pending or threatened litigation with quantified exposure
- [ ] Regulatory filings — material permits, licenses, approvals required for operations
- [ ] Corporate structure chart — legal entity structure, jurisdictions of incorporation
- [ ] Acquisition agreement (purchase agreement between sponsor and seller)
- [ ] Sponsor equity commitment letter

### Other Documents
- [ ] Phase I Environmental Site Assessment — for each material owned property
- [ ] Insurance summary — coverage types, limits, deductibles, key exclusions
- [ ] Real property schedule — owned and leased properties, lease terms, renewal options
- [ ] IT/cyber assessment — vulnerability assessment, incident history, data protection
- [ ] HR summary — headcount, compensation overview, benefit plans, union agreements
- [ ] Pension/OPEB obligations — funded status, projected contributions
- [ ] Tax structure memo — transaction tax structure, NOL analysis, Section 382 implications
- [ ] Intellectual property due diligence — freedom to operate, infringement risk

## Refinancing

### Financial Documents
- [ ] Most recent audited and interim financial statements
- [ ] Comparison: current performance vs original underwriting at time of initial credit facility
- [ ] Covenant compliance certificates — full history since original closing
- [ ] Updated financial projections
- [ ] Cash flow analysis — actual FCF generation during the life of the existing facility
- [ ] Debt schedule — current indebtedness, including any incremental facilities added post-closing
- [ ] Amendment and waiver history — all amendments, waivers, and consents since original closing

### Business Documents
- [ ] Business update since original transaction — material changes in strategy, operations, or market position
- [ ] Updated customer and revenue analysis — any material changes in concentration or mix
- [ ] Management changes since original closing

### Legal Documents
- [ ] Existing credit agreement — current, fully amended and restated version
- [ ] Proposed new credit agreement — term sheet or draft agreement
- [ ] Comparison of old vs new terms (covenant levels, baskets, pricing, maturity)
- [ ] Amendment history — all amendments with summary of changes

### Key Focus Areas for Refinancing
- Has the company performed in line with original underwriting?
- Why is the company refinancing (deleveraging achievement, market opportunity, maturity approaching, covenant pressure)?
- Are the new terms better or worse for lenders than the existing terms?
- What has changed since the original closing that affects credit quality?

## Add-On Acquisition

### All LBO Documents PLUS:

### Target Company Documents
- [ ] Target audited financial statements — 3 years (or available history if shorter)
- [ ] Target management accounts — trailing 12-18 months
- [ ] Target QoE report (if available)
- [ ] Target customer and revenue detail
- [ ] Target material contracts
- [ ] Target litigation summary
- [ ] Target environmental assessments
- [ ] Target employee roster and key personnel

### Combined Entity Analysis
- [ ] Synergy analysis — detail by category (cost, revenue), timing, one-time costs to achieve
- [ ] Integration plan — timeline, key milestones, management accountability, risk mitigation
- [ ] Combined entity pro forma financials — revenue, EBITDA, leverage, coverage
- [ ] Updated leverage analysis — pre-acquisition vs post-acquisition (with and without synergies)
- [ ] Sources and uses — acquisition funding detail
- [ ] Updated financial model — combined entity with synergy phase-in assumptions

### Key Focus Areas for Add-Ons
- Is the acquisition credit-accretive (reduces pro forma leverage) or credit-dilutive?
- Are synergies achievable and on what timeline?
- Does the platform management team have the bandwidth and expertise to integrate?
- What is the incremental debt burden and its impact on FCF and covenant headroom?
- Historical track record of the platform in integrating prior add-ons

## CRE (Commercial Real Estate)

### Property Documents
- [ ] Rent roll — current, detailed (tenant name, suite, SF, rent, escalations, lease dates, options)
- [ ] Lease abstracts — for all tenants above a materiality threshold (e.g., >5% of NRA or revenue)
- [ ] Full lease documents — for top 5-10 tenants and any tenants with unusual provisions
- [ ] Property condition report (PCR) — current physical condition, deferred maintenance, recommended repairs
- [ ] Phase I Environmental Site Assessment — for each property
- [ ] Appraisal — recent (within 6-12 months), compliant with FIRREA/USPAP standards
- [ ] Survey — ALTA/NSPS survey with all standard exceptions
- [ ] Site plan and floor plans
- [ ] Seismic assessment (if in seismic zone)
- [ ] ADA compliance assessment

### Financial Documents
- [ ] Historical operating statements — 3 years minimum, preferably audited
- [ ] Trailing 12 months (T-12) operating statement — month-by-month detail
- [ ] Current year budget
- [ ] Property tax bills — current and 2 prior years
- [ ] Insurance certificates and premium history — 3 years
- [ ] Capital expenditure history — 3 years, with detail by category
- [ ] Tenant improvement and leasing commission schedule — committed and projected
- [ ] Real estate tax assessment and appeal history

### Market Documents
- [ ] Market study — independent market analysis (CoStar, CBRE, JLL, or comparable)
- [ ] Comparable properties — rent comps, sale comps, vacancy comps
- [ ] New supply pipeline — projects under construction or planned within the competitive set
- [ ] Submarket vacancy and absorption data
- [ ] Cap rate trends for the property type and market

### Legal Documents
- [ ] Title report — preliminary title report with all exceptions reviewed
- [ ] Zoning confirmation — current zoning, permitted uses, any variances or special permits
- [ ] Entitlements — for development or value-add properties
- [ ] CC&Rs / HOA documents (if applicable)
- [ ] Ground lease (if applicable) — full lease with all amendments
- [ ] Parking agreements, easements, and operating agreements
- [ ] Property management agreement
- [ ] Borrower/guarantor organizational documents and financial statements

### Key Focus Areas for CRE
- Rent roll quality: are in-place rents at, above, or below market?
- Lease expiration concentration: material rollover risk in the next 24 months?
- Property condition: is the sponsor's capex budget adequate for required repairs?
- Market fundamentals: is the submarket stable, improving, or deteriorating?
- NOI sustainability: can current NOI be maintained, or is it inflated by above-market leases or temporary conditions?
- Borrower/guarantor strength: recourse provisions and guarantor financial capacity
