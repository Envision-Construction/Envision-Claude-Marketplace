# Pre-Investment Conflict of Interest Checklist

A comprehensive checklist for identifying, assessing, and documenting conflicts of interest before an investment decision. This review must be completed by the analyst and reviewed by the compliance officer before the credit is presented to the Investment Committee. Any identified conflict requires a documented determination — either clearance with rationale or escalation with a recommended mitigation plan.

---

## When This Checklist Applies

Complete this checklist for:
- Every new investment presented to IC for approval
- Every material position increase (defined as >25% of existing exposure or >1% of NAV)
- Any investment where a potential conflict has been flagged by any team member
- Any investment involving a counterparty, issuer, or sponsor with which the firm has an existing relationship
- Annual re-certification for all held positions (as part of the annual compliance review)

| 5.2 | Is any team member currently on the private side of an information wall for this issuer or any related entity? | [ ] | | [ ] Cleared / [ ] Wall Required |
| 5.3 | Has the firm participated in a lender group, ad hoc committee, or creditor meeting where MNPI was shared regarding this issuer? | [ ] | | [ ] Cleared / [ ] Wall Required |
| 5.4 | Has the firm signed a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) with the issuer, sponsor, or their advisors that could restrict trading? | [ ] | [Specify NDA terms and trading restrictions] | [ ] Cleared / [ ] Wall Required |
| 5.5 | Is there any public-side vs. private-side classification issue that needs to be resolved before the trade can be executed? | [ ] | | [ ] Cleared / [ ] Wall Required |

**MNPI Protocol:** If any MNPI question is answered "Yes," the compliance officer must immediately assess whether a trading restriction or information wall is required. No trade may be executed until the MNPI situation is resolved — either by the information becoming public, by cleansing the information (big-boy letter where applicable), or by waiting for the restricted period to lapse. All MNPI determinations must be documented in the firm's MNPI log.
