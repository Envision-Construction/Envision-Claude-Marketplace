## Description: <br>
Use when evaluating asset-based lending, equipment and leasing structures, or non-recourse / limited-recourse project finance, including borrowing-base facilities, residual-value underwriting, and project structures where repayment depends primarily on collateral value, contracted asset cash flows, or both. <br>
