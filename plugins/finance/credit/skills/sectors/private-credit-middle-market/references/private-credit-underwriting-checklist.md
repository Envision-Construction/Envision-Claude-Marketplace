## Private Credit Underwriting Checklist

Use this checklist for new private credit opportunities. Items marked with **(S)** are sponsor-backed only. Items marked with **(NS)** apply only to non-sponsored situations.

### 1. Borrower and Earnings Quality

- [ ] Business model and revenue drivers clearly understood
- [ ] Adjusted EBITDA reconciled to reported EBITDA with all add-backs explained
- [ ] Recurring capex, working capital needs, and cash taxes incorporated into lender cash flow
- [ ] Customer, supplier, and product concentration reviewed
- [ ] Revenue visibility and cyclicality assessed
- [ ] Quality of earnings work reviewed or gaps clearly identified

### 2. Ownership and Governance

- [ ] **(S)** Sponsor track record, support behavior, and amendment history assessed
- [ ] **(S)** Equity contribution and management alignment evaluated against current deal conventions
- [ ] **(S)** Value-creation plan reviewed for execution and leverage risk
- [ ] **(NS)** Key-person dependency assessed
- [ ] **(NS)** Reporting quality and governance substitution requirements identified
- [ ] **(NS)** Related-party transactions, owner compensation, and personal objectives reviewed

### 3. Structure and Terms

- [ ] Facility structure mapped: term loan, revolver, DDTL, unitranche, junior tranche, or other
- [ ] Total leverage, senior leverage, and full-draw leverage reviewed
- [ ] Equity cushion tested under downside valuation scenarios
- [ ] Undrawn commitments treated as real contingent exposure
- [ ] If unitranche, FOLO economics and AAL control rights reviewed
- [ ] If PIK exists, debt accretion and cash-pay conversion path modeled

### 4. Documentation and Control

- [ ] Covenant package fits the real business risks
- [ ] EBITDA definition reviewed for addback creep, pro forma latitude, and cure treatment
- [ ] Debt, lien, investment, and restricted payment baskets reviewed together for leakage risk
- [ ] Information rights and reporting cadence are sufficient for early intervention
- [ ] Change-of-control, guarantor-release, and collateral-release protections reviewed
- [ ] Amendment thresholds and likely negotiation leverage understood

### 5. Returns and Benchmarks

- [ ] Coupon, fees, OID, call protection, and expected life captured
- [ ] Gross return separated into cash yield, non-cash yield, and fee income where relevant
- [ ] Loss-adjusted return framed against realistic downside assumptions
- [ ] Current pricing and market norms sourced from `references/typical-deal-parameters.md`
- [ ] Current performance and loss benchmarks sourced from `references/private-credit-performance.md` and `references/default-recovery-rates.md`

### 6. Vehicle and Portfolio Fit

- [ ] Sector, single-name, and sponsor concentration implications identified
- [ ] Unfunded commitment burden incorporated into portfolio liquidity planning
- [ ] If held in a BDC, current numeric compliance sourced from `references/bdc-regulatory.md`
- [ ] Cross-asset comparison escalated to `portfolio-investment-process` when relevant

### 7. Risk Summary and Monitoring

- [ ] Top risks translated into specific mitigants or structural protections
- [ ] Downside path to covenant stress, amendment, or impairment identified
- [ ] Monitoring triggers defined with threshold, detection method, and frequency
- [ ] Handoff to `surveillance-monitoring` prepared for approved positions
