# Portfolio-Level IC Review Framework

Portfolio review should translate single-name surveillance into portfolio risk management. The purpose is to identify where issuer-level deterioration creates concentration, liquidity, mandate, or correlation problems that are invisible in isolated name-by-name reviews.

## Core Questions

The portfolio review should answer:
- where risk is concentrating through active decision or passive drift
- whether ratings, watchlist status, or maturities are clustering in specific sectors, sponsors, or structures
- whether a forced action in one holding could create second-order pressure elsewhere
- whether current portfolio positioning still matches mandate, liquidity needs, and conviction

## Suggested Review Sections

### Portfolio Snapshot
Summarize:
- exposure mix
- broad credit quality mix
- watchlist and stressed-name counts
- liquidity posture

### Concentration Review
Assess:
- single-name concentration
- sector and sub-sector drift
- rating-bucket concentration
- sponsor, geography, and structure overlap where relevant

Use `references/portfolio-risk-parameters.md` for current limits and soft triggers.

### Migration and Watchlist Review
Assess:
- where ratings are improving or deteriorating
- where negative outlooks or watch placements are clustering
- additions, removals, and category shifts on the watchlist
- whether several names are being driven by the same underlying macro or sector factor

### Risk and Liquidity Review
Assess:
- spread, duration, or expected-loss exposure
- maturity wall and refinancing concentration
- liquidity of the portfolio under ordinary and stressed conditions
- vehicle-specific tests or constraints where applicable

### Strategic Discussion
Use the review to decide:
- where exposure should be reduced or increased
- which sectors or structures now look crowded, impaired, or attractive
- whether new opportunities are displacing weaker holdings
- whether any stressed names now require formal IC attention

## Suggested Output Template

### Executive Summary
- overall portfolio health
- the few changes that matter most
- actions required before the next review

### Concentration Alerts
- limit breaches or areas approaching limits

### Watchlist and Escalation Summary
- names under greatest pressure
- what changed since the last review
- which names may soon force portfolio action

### Recommended Actions
- portfolio-level adjustment
- key position-level action
- strategic follow-up item
