# Credit Surveillance Checklists

Use these checklists as operating prompts, not rigid substitutes for judgment. The appropriate cadence and depth depend on the current surveillance tier, the asset class, and the quality of available information.

## Routine Monitoring Checklist

### Market and External Signals
- [ ] Record current price, spread, and rating status
- [ ] Note whether the move is issuer-specific or market-wide
- [ ] Check for material news, filings, or management changes
- [ ] Review sector headlines and peer developments

### Interim Operating Check
- [ ] Review any interim financial or management reporting available
- [ ] Note cash, liquidity, or revolver changes if disclosed
- [ ] Ask whether anything now challenges the base underwriting case

### Status Check
- [ ] Assign or confirm the current surveillance color and governance tier
- [ ] Compare current status to the last review
- [ ] If heightened monitoring is required, document why and what will be watched next

## Full Review Checklist

### Inputs
- [ ] Gather current financial statements and compliance materials
- [ ] Pull current market data and rating actions
- [ ] Review management commentary, filings, and major qualitative developments
- [ ] Refresh peer and sector context

### Financial and Structural Review
- [ ] Update the key financial metrics versus underwriting
- [ ] Refresh liquidity, leverage, and coverage
- [ ] Reconcile covenant compliance and forward cushion
- [ ] Review addbacks, amendments, and structural developments

### Market and Relative Value Review
- [ ] Compare current trading levels to purchase levels and peers
- [ ] Identify whether market pricing confirms or contradicts the internal view
- [ ] Disclose stale or low-quality marks where relevant

### Thesis Assessment
- [ ] Restate the original thesis briefly
- [ ] Identify what still holds and what changed
- [ ] Determine whether the base case, downside case, or action set has changed

### Deliverable
- [ ] Produce a concise surveillance summary
- [ ] State the current tier and rationale
- [ ] Give a clear action recommendation
- [ ] Identify what would force the next escalation or de-escalation step

## Deep Refresh Checklist

Use when a position needs a full annual-style reset, deep watchlist review, or pre-IC reassessment.

- [ ] Rebuild or materially refresh the financial and downside case
- [ ] Revisit relative value and alternative uses of capital
- [ ] Refresh industry, competitive, and structural context
- [ ] Reassess documentation quality and current risk transfer
- [ ] Update internal rating or risk score where applicable

## Structured Finance Overlay

For structured products, add:
- [ ] collateral performance review
- [ ] structural-test and cash-flow check
- [ ] concentration and migration review
- [ ] manager or servicer assessment
