# Worked Example: Quarterly Review & Escalation — NovaChem Industries

## Position Summary

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Issuer | NovaChem Industries, Inc. |
| Instrument | First Lien Term Loan B |
| Rating (entry) | B / B2 (S&P / Moody's) |
| Spread | SOFR + 450 bps |
| Position Size | $30M (1.5% of $2B portfolio) |
| Entry Price | 98.50 |
| Entry Date | January 2025 |
| Entry Leverage | 5.2x Total |
| Entry EBITDA | $110M LTM |
| Sector | Specialty Chemicals |

### Investment Thesis at Entry
NovaChem is a specialty chemical producer with leading positions in water treatment chemicals and industrial coatings. Thesis rests on (1) 70% recurring revenue from multi-year supply contracts, (2) margin stability from cost pass-through mechanisms, and (3) de-leveraging to below 4.5x within 24 months through EBITDA growth.

### Thesis-Kill Triggers (Set at Entry)
1. Leverage exceeds 6.5x
2. Loss of top-3 customer representing >10% of revenue
3. EBITDA margin compression >400bps from entry (entry margin: 16.2%)
4. Raw material cost pass-through mechanism failure (>2 consecutive quarters of margin erosion from input costs)

### Tier Assessment

| Indicator | Status | Trigger Breached |
|---|---|---|
| EBITDA vs. model | Orange | -18.2% exceeds Tier 2 threshold (>10%) |
| Leverage increase | Orange | +0.9x approaches Tier 2 threshold (>1.0x) |
| Covenant headroom | Orange | 8% below Tier 2 threshold (<10%) |
| Liquidity trend | Yellow | -$27M decline, but >$50M absolute |
| Market signal (price) | Orange | 94.00 is -450bps from entry |
| Thesis-kill proximity | Orange | Leverage at 6.1x vs. 6.5x kill trigger (within 10%) |

**Status Change: Yellow → Orange (Tier 2 — Team Review)**

### Root Cause Analysis
- Revenue decline driven by two factors: (1) industrial coatings volume down 12% on manufacturing slowdown, (2) water treatment contracts renewed at 5% lower pricing due to competitive pressure
- Raw material costs partially offset by lower input prices, but margin compression reflects operating deleverage on lower volumes
- Management reports pipeline remains stable but conversion timelines extending

### Actions Taken
- Escalated to portfolio team for Team Review (Tier 2)
- Position hedging evaluated: CDS not available (private credit). Considered reducing position by $10M — decision deferred pending Q1 earnings.
- Stress-tested downside: if EBITDA declines to $80M, leverage reaches 6.9x (breaches thesis-kill trigger #1)
- Flagged to events-distressed for preliminary recovery framework preparation

## Event Trigger — Orange to Red Consideration

### Event (February 2026)
NovaChem announces loss of Meridian Water Systems as a customer — second-largest customer representing 18% of total revenue. Meridian is bringing water treatment procurement in-house after acquiring a specialty chemical producer.

### Thesis-Kill Trigger Assessment

| Trigger | Threshold | Current Status |
|---|---|---|
| #1: Leverage >6.5x | 6.5x | At 6.1x (Q4), projected 7.2x post-customer loss — **BREACHED** |
| #2: Loss of top-3 customer >10% revenue | 10% | Meridian at 18% — **BREACHED** |
| #3: EBITDA margin compression >400bps | >400bps from 16.2% | Currently -200bps, projected -350bps — approaching |
| #4: Pass-through failure | 2+ consecutive quarters | Not triggered |

**Two thesis-kill triggers breached. Immediate escalation to Tier 3 — IC Notification.**

## IC Notification Package (Tier 3)

| Date | Event | Status |

### Current Position Assessment

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Current loan price | 89.00 |
| Unrealized loss | -$2.85M (-9.5% of par) |
| Projected LTM EBITDA (post-event) | $72M–$78M |
| Projected leverage | 7.0x–7.6x |
| Projected liquidity | $35M–$42M (6–8 months runway) |

### Recommended Action Options

| Option | Description | Estimated Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Hold** | Maintain $30M, monitor closely | Risk of further decline to 80s; recovery if replacement revenue secured |
| **Reduce** | Sell $15M at ~89.00 | Realize ~$1.4M loss, reduce exposure; may push price lower |
| **Hedge** | Not available (no CDS, private-side) | N/A |
| **Exit** | Sell full $30M at ~88.00–89.00 | Realize ~$3.0M loss; eliminates further downside |

**PM Recommendation**: Reduce position to $15M at current levels. Retain $15M with tightened monitoring for potential recovery if management secures replacement contracts within 2 quarters. Full exit if leverage exceeds 7.5x or liquidity falls below $30M.

### De-escalation Criteria (Return to Yellow)
1. Two consecutive quarters of EBITDA stabilization or improvement
2. Replacement of lost Meridian revenue with signed contracts covering >50% of lost volume
3. Leverage trajectory returning below 6.5x
4. Covenant headroom restored to >15%
